# Bio-based and advanced materials: knowledge file (updated 2026-11-25)

## Overview
Scope: forest-based materials, cellulose and lignin products, bio-based chemicals, advanced and circular materials. Europe and Finland focus. The Finnish forest industry (Stora Enso, UPM, Metsä, Kemira) anchors the sector. Aalto and VTT run the FinnCERES flagship and the Aalto Bioinnovation Centre ([Aalto, FinnCERES](https://www.aalto.fi/en/collaboration/finnceres)). Current state: pulp and fibre markets are weak and costs are inflated, so incumbents are restructuring portfolios (UPM spun off plywood as WISA Group, Stora Enso is demerging Swedish forests), cutting risky textile-fibre projects and investing in packaging and biochemicals. Policy and EU funding remain supportive. Aalto-linked start-ups keep raising small rounds (lignin, cellulose, foams).

## Trends
- Incumbents simplify and refocus on packaging, energy and biochemicals.
  - UPM: plywood demerger registered 2026-10-31, WISA Group trading on Nasdaq Helsinki from 2026-11-02 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-partial-demerger-of-upm-kymmene-corporation-has-been-registered-wisa-group-plc-to-be-listed-on-nasdaq-helsinki-302925755.html)). Leuna wood-to-chemicals biorefinery (220,000 t a year) inaugurated 2026-10-19 ([Chemeurope](https://www.chemeurope.com/en/news/1190108/from-wood-to-chemicals-upm-inaugurates-pioneering-biorefinery-in-germany.html); search summaries only).
  - Stora Enso: board approved demerger of Swedish forest assets 2026-11-03, EGM expected 2026-12-15, listing reported for March 2027 ([Pulpapernews](https://www.pulpapernews.com/20261103/18344/stora-enso-approves-demerger-plan-its-swedish-forest-assets-business)). On 2026-11-10 it decided to keep Central European sawmills and building solutions ([PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/stora-enso-concludes-strategic-review-for-central-european-sawmills-and-building-solutions-operations-302934841.html)). Cellufoam pilot reported Sept 2026 (one secondary source; verify).
- Metsä cancelled the Kuura textile fibre mill on 2026-08-11 and is investing about EUR 25 million in a Muoto fibre-packaging line at Äänekoski (start 2028) ([Metsä Muoto/Kuura](https://chemxplore.com/news/metsa-group-muoto-fibre-packaging-investment)); lignin and carbon-capture work continues ([Packaging Insights](https://www.packaginginsights.com/news/metsa-pivots-textiles-fiber-food-packaging.html)). Rauma CO2 capture (100,000 t a year, EUR 70 million) got EUR 21 million aid in September 2026; investment decision not before early 2027 ([Metsä Group](https://www.metsagroup.com/news-and-publications/news/2026/metsa-groups-carbon-dioxide-capture-plant-receives-eur-21-million-in-aid/)).
- Textile-fibre start-ups face capital pressure. Spinnova plans a US IPO of at least USD 15 million (market cap about EUR 27 million) ([Inderes](https://www.inderes.dk/en/analyst-comments/spinnova-announces-intention-to-pursue-us-ipo-and-dual-listing)); SEC registration statement filed 2026-08-27 with the offering expected September–October. A 2026-11-25 search again found no pricing or listing, so it has slipped. A EUR 1.5 million bridge loan to partner Tearfil was agreed 2026-09-30 (forum mentions only, unverified). On 2026-11-05 it signed a non-binding LOI with Outlast ([Spinnova/Outlast](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/accwirecq-2026-11-5-spinnova-signs-letter-of-intent-with-outlast-to-develop-spinnovar-fibre-for-insulation-and-temperature-regulation)).
- Lignin valorisation is attracting industrial investors (TekVentures, Kiilto, Metsä Spring); UPM Leuna and altM point the same way.
- Plastic-free barrier coatings for fibre packaging win recognition: Xampla and Huhtamaki won a Packaging Europe Sustainability Award ([Packaging Europe](https://www.packagingeurope.com/winners-of-the-sustainability-awards-2026-announced/)). Cellulose barrier films (VTT/LUT F3) near commercial use.
- Cost inflation weighs on chemicals (Kemira) and legacy paper; graphic paper keeps shrinking (reported -7.2 % in 2025, search summary). Softwood pulp demand expected subdued (Metsä).
- Bio-based foams, insulation and packaging are typical Aalto commercialisation targets.

## Regulation and policy
- EU Bioeconomy Strategy adopted 2025-11-27 (COM(2025) 960): support for bio-based plastics under PPWR, possible bio-based content requirements, Circular Economy Act to support bio-waste valorisation. [Commission](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/document/download/dbf8d2ba-9332-4f7a-b336-f356fa4b7236_en?filename=COM_2025_960_1_EN_ACT_part1_v10_0.pdf) Council conclusions 2026-03-17 (one secondary source).
- Bio-based content under PPWR is not yet binding. No Circular Economy Act proposal text found as of 2026-11-19 (not rechecked this run).
- Biotech Act II: call for evidence closed 2026-06-10; proposal pending.
- Cefic (June 2026) wants one biomass-content target per product group counting bio-attributed inputs.
- Finland's reverse auction for biogenic CO2 capture aid (EUR 90 million reserved; EUR 26.6 million granted to three projects in September 2026, search summary).
