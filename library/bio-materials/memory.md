# Bio-based and advanced materials: knowledge file (updated 2026-11-16)

## Overview
Scope: forest-based materials, cellulose and lignin products, bio-based chemicals, advanced and circular materials. Europe and Finland focus. The Finnish forest industry (Stora Enso, UPM, Metsä, Kemira) anchors the sector. Aalto and VTT run the FinnCERES flagship and the Aalto Bioinnovation Centre ([Aalto, FinnCERES](https://www.aalto.fi/en/collaboration/finnceres)). Current state: markets for pulp and fibre are weak and costs are inflated, so incumbents are restructuring portfolios (UPM spun off plywood as WISA Group, Stora Enso is demerging Swedish forests), cutting risky textile-fibre projects and investing in packaging and biochemicals. Policy and EU funding remain supportive. Aalto-linked start-ups keep raising small rounds (lignin, cellulose, foams).

## Trends
- Incumbents are simplifying and refocusing on packaging, energy and biochemicals.
  - UPM: the plywood demerger was registered on 2026-10-31 and WISA Group shares were due to start trading on Nasdaq Helsinki on 2026-11-02. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-partial-demerger-of-upm-kymmene-corporation-has-been-registered-wisa-group-plc-to-be-listed-on-nasdaq-helsinki-302925755.html)
  - UPM inaugurated the Leuna wood-to-chemicals biorefinery (220,000 tonnes a year) on 2026-10-19; lignin-derived speciality chemicals are reported in stable operation with sales ramping up (search summaries only). [Chemeurope](https://www.chemeurope.com/en/news/1190108/from-wood-to-chemicals-upm-inaugurates-pioneering-biorefinery-in-germany.html)
  - Stora Enso: the board approved the demerger plan for the Swedish forest assets (Bergslagets Skogar AB) on 2026-11-03; EGM expected 2026-12-15; listing on Nasdaq Stockholm and Helsinki, reported for March 2027. [Pulpapernews](https://www.pulpapernews.com/20261103/18344/stora-enso-approves-demerger-plan-its-swedish-forest-assets-business) On 2026-11-10 it decided to keep its Central European sawmills and building solutions business after a strategic review. [PR Newswire](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/stora-enso-concludes-strategic-review-for-central-european-sawmills-and-building-solutions-operations-302934841.html) It opened a Cellufoam fibre-foam packaging pilot plant in September 2026 (one secondary search summary; verify).
- Metsä cancelled the Kuura textile fibre mill (Kemi) on 2026-08-11 because of Asian overcapacity and low willingness to pay a premium, and is investing about EUR 25 million in a commercial Muoto fibre-packaging line at Äänekoski (start 2028). [Metsä Muoto/Kuura](https://chemxplore.com/news/metsa-group-muoto-fibre-packaging-investment) Metsä's lignin and carbon capture projects continue ([Packaging Insights](https://www.packaginginsights.com/news/metsa-pivots-textiles-fiber-food-packaging.html)). The Rauma biogenic CO2 capture plant (100,000 tonnes a year, total cost EUR 70 million) was awarded EUR 21 million in state aid in September 2026; the investment decision is not expected before early 2027. [Metsä Group](https://www.metsagroup.com/news-and-publications/news/2026/metsa-groups-carbon-dioxide-capture-plant-receives-eur-21-million-in-aid/)
- Textile-fibre start-ups face capital pressure: Spinnova plans a US IPO of at least USD 15 million against a market cap of about EUR 27 million. [Inderes](https://www.inderes.dk/en/analyst-comments/spinnova-announces-intention-to-pursue-us-ipo-and-dual-listing) It applied for a Nasdaq Capital Market listing on 2026-09-22 and expected completion in Q4 2026; a EUR 1.5 million bridge loan to partner Tearfil was agreed on 2026-09-30; no pricing or closing had been found by 2026-11-16. [Inderes, Spinnova](https://www.inderes.fi/en/companies/Spinnova) On 2026-11-05 it signed a non-binding letter of intent with Outlast for insulation and temperature-regulating textiles, a move towards technical applications. [Spinnova/Outlast](https://www.financialcontent.com/article/accwirecq-2026-11-5-spinnova-signs-letter-of-intent-with-outlast-to-develop-spinnovar-fibre-for-insulation-and-temperature-regulation)
- Lignin valorisation is attracting industrial investors (Teknor Apex's TekVentures, Kiilto) for adhesives and coatings; UPM's Leuna output and altM's round point the same way.
- Plastic-free barrier coatings for fibre packaging are winning recognition: Xampla and Huhtamaki won a Packaging Europe Sustainability Award at the Sustainable Packaging Summit (10–12 November 2026). [Packaging Europe](https://www.packagingeurope.com/winners-of-the-sustainability-awards-2026-announced/) Woamy and Sulapac were reported as finalists in a packaging competition there; result not found.
- Fully cellulose-based barrier films and coatings (VTT/LUT F3 project) are nearing commercial use in recyclable packaging.
- Raw-material and energy cost inflation weighs on chemicals (Kemira) and legacy paper and fibre (UPM). Paperboard balance is improving and softwood pulp demand is expected to stay subdued (Metsä). Graphic paper keeps shrinking (reported -7.2 % in 2025, Cepi via search summary).
- Bio-based foams, insulation and packaging are the typical Aalto commercialisation targets.

## Regulation and policy
- EU Bioeconomy Strategy adopted 2025-11-27 (COM(2025) 960). It says the Commission will support recognition and uptake of bio-based plastics under PPWR and consider bio-based content requirements; the coming Circular Economy Act is to support bio-waste collection and valorisation. [Commission, COM(2025) 960](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/document/download/dbf8d2ba-9332-4f7a-b336-f356fa4b7236_en?filename=COM_2025_960_1_EN_ACT_part1_v10_0.pdf) The Council approved conclusions on 2026-03-17 (one secondary source).
- Bio-based content under PPWR is not yet binding. A 2026 market report describes Article 8 as the only enacted provision addressing bio-based feedstock in plastics (search summary). No Circular Economy Act proposal text was found as of 2026-11-16.
- Biotech Act II: call for evidence closed 2026-06-10; proposal pending.
- Cefic (June 2026) wants one biomass-content target per product group that counts bio-attributed inputs. Bio-based advocates may disagree.
- Finland ran a reverse auction for biogenic CO2 capture aid (EUR 90 million reserved; EUR 26.6 million granted to three projects in September 2026, via search summary).
