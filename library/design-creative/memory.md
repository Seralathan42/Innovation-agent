# Design and creative industries: knowledge file (updated 2026-11-25)

## Overview
Scope: design-driven products, games, media technology, fashion and textile innovation. Europe and Finland focus.

- **Games** are Finland's largest commercial creative export.
- **Textile and fashion innovation** is shaped by EU circularity policy.
- **Research-based ventures** face a tight games funding market but have grant openings in textiles, media technology and the creative economy.

State in late November 2026:
- The two listed Finnish bellwethers reported growth (Remedy, Marimekko), while smaller game studios keep closing.
- The Commission adopted the Digital Fairness Act (DFA) proposal on 2026-11-11. It targets in-game currencies and pay-to-win mechanics for minors and backs enforcement with fines of up to 6% of turnover.
- Textile innovation is still pre-commercial (Spinnova, Infinited Fiber) but has the best-fitting open EU grant.

  - Finland: turnover €7.08 bn (1.79%), value added €3.0 bn (2.88%).

## Regulation and policy
- **Digital Fairness Act**: adopted by the Commission as a proposal on 2026-11-11 with a Simplification Directive and a Consumer Protection Cooperation (CPC) Regulation revision ([Commission DFA page](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/consumer-protection-law/digital-fairness-act_en); [press release via EuropaWire](https://news.europawire.eu/european-commission-proposes-digital-fairness-act-and-stronger-eu-consumer-law-enforcement/eu-press-release/2026/11/12/15/28/04/187330/)).
  - **Targets**: dark patterns, addictive design (consumers can switch it off), overspending prompts, unfair personalisation, influencers, dynamic pricing, subscription traps, no human contact in customer service.
  - **Games provisions**: clearer real-money pricing for virtual currencies; a ban on gambling-like features for minors such as pay-to-win; limits on personalised ads and prices aimed at minors.
  - **Enforcement**: fines up to 6% of worldwide turnover in large cross-border cases.
  - **Legal form**: trackers list it as a regulation, COM(2026) 1100, procedure 2026/0356(COD) ([MediaLaws tracker](https://www.medialaws.eu/digital-fairness-act-legislative-tracker/); not confirmed on the Commission page).
  - **Readings differ**: press says in-game currency off by default for under-18s and paid loot boxes banned for minors ([VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/articles/174761-digital-fairness-act-loot-boxes.html)). Neogames reads it as a ban on all in-game purchases by under-18s ([Neogames statement](https://neogames.fi/statement-new-eu-regulation-threatens-the-finnish-game-industry-and-the-european-game-industry/)).
  - **Next steps**: Parliament and Council; no application date yet.
- **Textile EPR**: via the revised Waste Framework Directive, in force 2025-10-16. Transposition due about June 2027, schemes about April 2028 (sources conflict). Fees are eco-modulated. [EURATEX](https://euratex.eu/sustainable-businesses/circular-economy/textiles-epr-europe/)
- **Ecodesign and Digital Product Passport for textiles** (from compliance-vendor summaries, not EU documents; verify): the delegated act was not adopted as of autumn 2026, indicatively expected in H2 2027. A passport registry reportedly went live 2026-07-20. A ban on destroying unsold apparel reportedly applies to large companies since July 2026. The MAT-PROD-08 call text confirms recyclability, recycled content and footprint are expected in the requirements ([EU portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-cl4-2027-01-mat-prod-08)).
- **Circular Economy Act**: expected; status unverified.
- **National EPR schemes**: France and the Netherlands said to be active (single vendor source, unverified).
- **AI and copyright**: the AI4Creatives call names copyright infrastructure for licensing content for AI as an EU aim ([EU portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-cl2-2027-01-heritage-02)). AI Act copyright rules not researched.

## Opportunities for Aalto inventions
- **Textile and material innovation** is the strongest fit.
  - HORIZON-CL4-2027-01-MAT-PROD-08 is open until 2027-02-02: €16 m, about 3 grants of €4–6 m, TRL 5 to 6–7. Start-up involvement encouraged; 10–25% of the budget passed to SMEs at up to €150,000 each.
  - Aalto has fresh patent filings (EPO search: 8 Aalto filings since 2025 on textile, fibre or cellulose): elastane detection with VTT ([FI20236268A1](https://worldwide.espacenet.com/patent/search?q=pn%3DFI20236268A1)); cellulose and polyester blend separation ([CN121065838A](https://worldwide.espacenet.com/patent/search?q=pn%3DCN121065838A)); ionic liquid purification, cellulose aerogels, bio-based cushioning.
  - HERITAGE-03 Crafting routes to a circular economy (opens 2027-05-13, due 2027-09-23, €10.5 m) fits design-led circularity.
- **Design research as an entry point to industry**: MELANGE (Aalto-led, €4 m, with Lenzing and mills) is a model ([Aalto](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/design-at-the-start-of-the-supply-chain-aalto-university-leads-a-major-eu-project-to-transform)). Woamy and Secto Design show design brands can be investors and customers.
- **Media technology and AI tools for creators**: Creative Europe MEDIA Innovative tools (due 2027-01-14, €14 m); Creative Innovation Lab (2027-04-22, €7.18 m); AI4Creatives (2027-09-23, €5 m); HERITAGE-05 (2027-09-23, €10.5 m); HERITAGE-09 (€20 m; stages 2027-05-04 and 2027-09-30); EU AI Media Platform (2027-02-16, one €10 m grant, suits large consortia).
- **XR and immersive hardware**: HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-HUMAN-01, due 2027-03-18, €39 m, about 8 grants, TRL 3–5.
- **Games**: Creative Europe video games call (due 2027-02-02, €13.5 m) is for companies. Weak VC makes licensing to studios or tool positioning more realistic than studio spin-offs. The DFA may raise demand for compliant monetisation design, age-assurance and parental-control tooling and dark-pattern research (inference only; timeline years).
- **NEB**: 2026 topics close 2026-12-01 (€10 m, 2 grants; six days left on 2026-11-25, so only realistic for consortia already formed). 2027 topics open 2027-05-05, close 2027-12-01 (e.g. €10.5 m intergenerational neighbourhoods).
- **Deep-tech route**: EIC Accelerator 2027 cut-offs 20 Jan, 17 Mar, 1 Sep, 4 Nov ([EIC](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en)); EIC Transition due 2027-09-14, €92 m ([EIC](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-transition_en)). EIC Pathfinder dates are from secondary sources only.
- **Finnish funders**:
  - Research to Business call 2/2026 ran 8 June–16 September 2026; decisions targeted January 2027; 80% of costs, typically €400,000–700,000 ([Business Finland](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/r2b-funding-call-2-2026/)). Call 1/2026 ran 1 December 2025–4 March 2026 ([Business Finland](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/research-to-business--funding-call-12026/)). No 2027 call published as of 2026-11-25; a new call around December is likely (inference from the 2026 pattern).
  - Creative industries Co-Innovation call 2026 (€9 m) closed 2026-04-29 ([Business Finland](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/funding-call-co-innovation-creative-industries/)). No 2027 call found.
  - Rise to Challenge 2026 exists; six projects were funded (status of a 2027 call not checked). Research Council of Finland calls not checked.
- **Spin-out models**: Woamy and Ioncell (materials-led); Spinnova's brand and fibre partnerships show a textile route, and its slow path to revenue is a caution on scale-up time and capital.
