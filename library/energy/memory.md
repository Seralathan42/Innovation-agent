# Energy and storage: knowledge file (first run 2026-11-05, updated 2026-11-09)

## Overview
Scope: batteries and storage, renewables, hydrogen, smart grids and district heating, with a focus on Europe and Finland. Grid-scale battery storage is the fastest-moving area. Finland has a high renewable share and a fast-growing storage pipeline, and its district heating sector is adopting thermal storage and heat pumps. Hydrogen support exists (EU Hydrogen Bank) but delivery is uncertain.

## Market size and growth
- Europe installed 36 GWh of battery storage in 2025 (+48% YoY). The fleet is now above 100 GWh. Utility-scale was over 50% of installs, and residential fell to 34% from 49% ([SolarPower Europe via ESS News, 2026](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/06/23/solarpower-europe-reports-record-36-gwh-of-european-storage-installs/)).
- Forecast: more than 50 GWh in 2026 and 138 GWh in 2030, a 28% CAGR over 2026-30 (SolarPower Europe, medium scenario). EUPD Research is higher at 57 GWh for 2026 (+78%), so the forecasts differ ([ESS News, 2026](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/09/07/europes-storage-market-to-hit-57-gwh-in-2026-says-eupd-research/)).
- Finland: Fingrid reported about 1,050 MW of storage in February 2026 (980 MW from 261 systems of 1 MW or more, plus about 70 MW smaller) ([Fingrid, 2026](https://www.fingrid.fi/en/news/news/2026/the-combined-capacity-of-electricity-storages-in-finland-now-exceeds-1000-mw/)). Renewables Finland reports just over 1.6 GW in July, with nearly 1 GW under construction and 11.9 GW planned ([Renewables Finland, 2026](https://suomenuusiutuvat.fi/?p=15373)). Definitions differ, so treat the numbers with care.
- Renewable share of final energy consumption in 2025: Finland 53.0%, EU27 26.2% ([Eurostat nrg_ind_ren](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/nrg_ind_ren/default/table)).
- Hydrogen: the third Hydrogen Bank auction (IF25, results 2026-05-07) awarded about EUR 1.09 billion to 9 projects, about 1.1 GW electrolysis ([Hydrogen Europe, 2026](https://hydrogeneurope.eu/high-levels-of-competition-in-3rd-eu-hydrogen-bank-auction/)). Sources differ on the lowest bid (EUR 0.44/kg vs a EUR 0.57/kg range floor); unresolved.
- Not found: reliable market-size figures in euros for hydrogen, district heating and smart grids. No company-count statistics were collected. Eurostat was not re-queried this run.

- International investors and suppliers: Foresight (Nivala BESS project), Sungrow (supplier).
- Aalto spin-offs: Halide Energy (copper flow batteries), Automous Oy (storage optimisation SaaS, pre-seed EUR 575,000, [Aalto Startup Center](https://startupcenter.aalto.fi/automous-oy-secures-a-pre-seed-investment-round-of-575000eu)).

## Trends
- Storage is moving from residential to utility-scale. Finnish projects are shifting to 2-hour systems.
- Developers sell ready-to-build projects to infrastructure funds and utilities (Fu-Gen to Nala), and lenders are financing Finnish projects (Delta, 600 MWh). Construction starts in autumn 2026: Delta Kärppiö (125 MW / 300 MWh, 2026-09-25, [ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/09/25/delta-capacity-advances-second-major-finland-bess-this-month-with-sungrow-supply-deal/)) and Nala Vuolijoki (125 MW / 250 MWh, 2026-10-08, [ESS News](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/10/08/nala-renewables-starts-construction-on-250-mwh-finland-bess/)). AKKU One (Delta/Covenergo, 300 MWh) targets Q4 2027.
- Grid-forming capability is becoming a requirement (Fingrid, October 2026).
- Thermal storage for district heating is scaling: Polar Night's 250 MWh unit for Lahti Energia is due summer 2027, and a power-to-heat-to-power pilot is planned in Valkeakoski.
- Software and VPP/optimisation platforms are appearing around the batteries.
- Hydrogen subsidies show delivery risk: only 6 of 15 selected second-round projects signed grants ([CINEA, 2026](https://cinea.ec.europa.eu/news-events/news/six-winners-2024-innovation-fund-hydrogen-auction-sign-grant-agreements-advancing-renewable-hydrogen-2026-01-20_en)).

## Regulation and policy
- Fingrid's grid-forming requirement for new wind and solar in weak grid areas, met with co-located batteries sized at ±10%, ±20% or ±30% of plant capacity ([ESS News, 2026](https://www.ess-news.com/2026/10/15/finland-demands-grid-forming-support-for-new-wind-solar-in-weak-grids/)).
- European Grids Package: the Council adopted its general approach on 2026-06-26. Parliament's position and trilogues are unconfirmed. Industry says storage and flexibility are underplayed. A first tripartite agreement on storage was signed with member states committing to remove barriers and enable cost-reflective tariffs ([SolarPower Europe, 2026](https://www.solarpowereurope.org/press-releases/energy-council-sets-direction-on-grids-package-as-storage-tripartite-agreement-is-signed)). Verify the current status.
- SolarPower Europe is calling for an EU Battery Storage Action Plan. This is a request, not adopted policy.
- European Hydrogen Bank: three auctions run; the Commission has reportedly not committed to auctions beyond the current round (single source).
- Not researched: the EU Batteries Regulation details, RED III transposition and Finnish grid fee rules.
