# Quantum technology (hardware, software, sensing, cryogenics) – knowledge file, updated 2026-11-25

## Overview
Europe has a strong quantum research base and a growing industry. Finland is a hub through IQM (superconducting computers), Bluefors (cryogenics) and software firms (QMill; Algorithmiq, Finnish-founded), with Aalto, VTT and InstituteQ as the academic core. IQM listed on Nasdaq (ticker IQMX) and Nasdaq Helsinki in July 2026; Dealroom gives the listing valuation as $1.9bn ([Dealroom, 2026](https://dealroom.co/charts/finland-startups-2026)). Bluefors became a unicorn in September 2026 ([Dealroom, 2026-09-23](https://dealroom.co/news/155743-bluefors-joins-finlands-unicorn-club-with-a-1b-valuation-and-217m-in-rev/)). Finland's national roadmap 2025-2035 targets €3bn sector turnover, €400M private investment and 10,000 jobs by 2035, and InstituteQ aims to onboard 50 end-user companies by 2030 (secondary source: [Quantum Zeitgeist, 2026-11-10](https://quantumzeitgeist.com/aalto-university-finlands-otanano-offers-quantum/)).

Finnish Trade Register lookups work for single company names (they fail on the bare word "quantum"). Verified as registered and active (earlier run): IQM Quantum Computers Oyj (Espoo, 2018), Bluefors Oy (Helsinki, 2008), Semiqon Technologies Oy (Espoo, 2022), QMill Oy (Espoo, 2024), Arctic Instruments Oy (Espoo, 2024), Coherence Quantum Oy (Espoo, registered 2026-06-22) and Algorithmiq Oy (Helsinki, 2020). There is no industry code for quantum, so a reliable count of Finnish quantum companies is still not available.

## Market size and growth
- Global: start-up investment in quantum technology reached $12.6bn in 2025, 6.3x 2024, about 90% of it into quantum computing; public sources fell to about 3% of the total. Quantum computing company revenue passed $1bn for the first time in 2025. McKinsey's 2035 market estimate is $60-100bn ([McKinsey Quantum Technology Monitor 2026](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-technology/our-insights/mckinsey-quantum-technology-monitor-2026-a-commercial-tipping-point)). Caveat: read through search summaries, not the full report.
- No official statistics exist for quantum as a sector: Eurostat has no quantum product or industry code. As context only, Finnish business R&D spending was €6,039M in 2024 (€5,703M in 2023, €5,397M in 2022) and EU27 €270.6bn in 2024 ([Eurostat rd_e_berdindr2](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/rd_e_berdindr2/default/table); earlier cited from [htec_sti_exp2](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/htec_sti_exp2/default/table)). Not re-queried this run.
- Finnish venture context: Finnish start-ups raised $1.9bn in 110 rounds in January-September 2026; ICEYE's €1bn round is 62% of that, leaving $724M without it. Deep tech took 74% of the money (33% without ICEYE). Finland has 13 unicorns ([Dealroom, 2026](https://dealroom.co/charts/finland-startups-2026)).
- IQM Q3 2026, from the primary filing ([IQM 6-K, SEC, 2026-11-05](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002113060/000119312526448359/iqmx-ex99_1.htm)): Q3 revenue €8.86M (+51% from €5.88M); Jan-Sep revenue €17.73M (+49%); Q3 operating loss €171.2M including a €127.1M non-cash share listing expense; Jan-Sep operating loss €231.8M; new orders Q3 €42.2M (incl. €33M CSC LUMI AI Factory), year to date €52.9M; backlog €102.5M (+52% since start of year); cash €260.4M (down from €309.4M after listing); 31 systems sold, 17 delivered, 17 in production; 450+ employees. ## Trends
- Quantum computers integrated into HPC/AI factories (IQM–CSC €33M).
- First private-enterprise purchases (Galaxy, Poland, 54 qubits, Q4 2026).
- Capacity scale-up: IQM Espoo fab over €40M, up to 30 systems/year; first 150-qubit systems at customer sites; error-correction demonstrators planned for 2027.
- Error correction at hardware level attracts first-round money (Quint, €9.1M); IQM patents in the same area.
- Control software draws late-stage capital (Q-CTRL, $290M).
- Cryogenics supply chain: Bluefors partners with Xanadu on compact data-centre cryogenics ([Xanadu Q3 release, SEC, 2026-11-11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002097163/000209716326000025/exhibit991pressrelease-nov.htm)); new cryo-control start-ups (Coherence Quantum).
- Pilot lines include universities and institutes: Pasqal-led €50M Q-PLANET (Chips JU, 28 partners, VTT among them) ([The Quantum Insider, 2026-10-06](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/10/06/pasqal-50-million-q-planet-neutral-atom-quantum/)); QuiX Quantum PhotonDelta project, 100+ qubit photonic computer with three Dutch universities, 2026-2028 ([HPCwire, 2026-11-13](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/quix-quantum-secures-photondelta-funding-for-photonic-quantum-computing/)).
- European sovereignty is becoming procurement policy (Quantum Act draft).
- Telecom use cases (Telia–QMill). Nordic Quantum Alliance (June 2026).
- Early-stage rounds are small (€2.8M to €12M) versus IQM, so spin-offs need EU and EIC money; Quint's VC plus EIC Pathfinder is a template.

## Regulation and policy
- EU Quantum Europe Strategy (July 2025). The Quantum Act was planned for Q2 2026 ([Parliament legislative train](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/legislative-train/theme-a-new-plan-for-europe-s-sustainable-prosperity-and-competitiveness/file-quantum-act)) and slipped. It was reportedly scheduled for 18 November 2026 ([Technology.org, 2026-11-14](https://www.technology.org/2026/11/14/draft-eu-quantum-act-european-suppliers-logical-qubits-2030/)). A search on 2026-11-25 again found no Commission proposal or press release; the latest item is the leaked draft ([The Quantum Insider, 2026-11-10](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/11/10/draft-eu-quantum-act-would-favor-european-suppliers-set-computing-and-security-targets/)). An MLex report says internal delays could push the proposal into 2027 ([MLex](https://www.mlex.com/mlex/amp/articles/2521171)). Status: not adopted as far as can be verified. Briefing: [Abreu Advogados, 2026-10-14](https://abreuadvogados.com/en/blogs/european-commission-prepares-proposal-for-a-regulation-to-boost-quantum-technologies-in-europe/).
- Leaked draft (via The Quantum Insider, summarised by [MLQ.ai, 2026-11-10](https://mlq.ai/news/draft-eu-quantum-act-would-reserve-public-quantum-purchases-for-eu-controlled-companies/)):
  - Public procurement and strategic projects generally limited to EU-controlled companies; non-EU suppliers need reciprocal partnership or Commission authorisation.
  - Critical-sector procurement must be post-quantum ready and crypto-agile six months after entry into force.
  - Commission list of critical quantum components; monitoring of foreign acquisitions; a Quantum Strategy Board of member-state representatives.
  - Targets: ~100 logical qubits per system by 2030, thousands by 2035; metropolitan quantum networks by 2030; foundries 2030-2035; a European Quantum Service Platform for EuroHPC quantum computers.
  - No budget; funding depends on the proposed European Competitiveness Fund plus national and private money.
  - Text unfinished and may change.
- EuroHPC calls that closed 2026-11-17 (pilot lines, testing infrastructure, neutral atoms, QKD) have passed. Press reported six EuroHPC quantum calls totalling €119M ([HPCwire](https://www.hpcwire.com/off-the-wire/eurohpc-launches-6-quantum-calls-with-e119m-in-funding/)).

## Opportunities for Aalto inventions
- If the Quantum Act is proposed as leaked, EU-controlled suppliers get procurement preference. That favours Aalto spin-offs selling into European quantum computers and networks; plan ownership structure (EU control) when raising from non-EU investors. The delay means no certainty before 2027.
- Realistic near-term targets: Xecs Quantum (preliminary 26 Nov 2026, final 5 Mar 2027), Business Finland research-SME cooperation (18 Dec 2026), QML (2027-01-28) and photonics (2027-03-18).
- Cryogenic components, readout electronics, amplifiers and qubit materials: sell into IQM, Bluefors and Coherence Quantum supply chains. Bluefors is profitable and moving to compact data-centre cryogenics with Xanadu. Aalto's thermal-noise attenuator filing (WO2025181435A2) sits here.
- Error-suppressing qubit designs and error-correction hardware: first-round investors pay for this (Quint), IQM patents actively, Aalto's QCD group works in the field ([Aalto QCD](https://www.aalto.fi/en/department-of-applied-physics/quantum-computing-and-devices-qcd); [Aalto news](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/mikko-mottonen-and-his-team-aim-to-rein-in-qubit-errors)). Do freedom-to-operate checks against IQM early.
- Quantum software and algorithms (chemistry, telecom, optimisation, QML); financing via Quantonation, Vsquared, Lifeline Ventures, EIC and Scaleup Europe. Q-CTRL shows the category scales but raises the bar.
- Quantum sensing: space gravimetry call has an area for emerging technologies such as SQUIDs at TRL 4-5, fitting Finnish superconducting-sensor skills. PQC and network protocols via CL3 calls.
- OtaNano as a prototyping base open to companies ([Aalto InstituteQ](https://www.aalto.fi/en/instituteq); [InstituteQ events](https://instituteq.fi/quantum-software-day-2026/)). The Finnish Quantum Flagship produced three spin-outs in two years (A-Gate Sensors, Arctic Instruments, QMill); Coherence Quantum came from Aalto QCD in 2026. IQM commercialises Aalto and VTT research ([VTT](https://www.vttresearch.com/en/news-and-ideas/quantum-computing-iqm-commercialise-groundbreaking-research-aalto-university-and-vtt)).
- Research to Business remains the main Finnish route to a spin-off; next call expected around early December 2026 (inference).
