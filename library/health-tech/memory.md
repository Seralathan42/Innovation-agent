# Health technology: knowledge file (updated 2026-11-09)

## Overview
Scope: medical devices, diagnostics and imaging, digital health and brain technology, with a focus on Europe and Finland. Finland has a strong cluster in wearables (Oura), brain stimulation (Nexstim, an Aalto-launched company, [Aalto](https://aalto.fi/en/news/aalto-university-launched-brain-stimulation-company-lists-on-the-stock-exchange)), monitoring (Bittium), eye diagnostics (Revenio) and dental imaging (Planmeca). Public support is large: Business Finland's Health 360 Finland programme, Research Council of Finland clinical and Proof of Concept calls, and Horizon Europe.

## Key players
Finland: Oura, Nexstim (154 systems installed at Q3 2026, 57 in the US), Bittium, Revenio, Planmeca, Gosta Labs (EUR 7.5 million seed), Medicubex (EUR 4 million contract), Soihtu DTx. Nexstim collaborates with Brainlab on neurosurgery.
Aalto spin-offs and projects: RoboMed (robotic diagnostic capsule), Cornea Sense (corneal hydration, over EUR 1.1 million pre-seed, [Startup Researcher](https://www.startupresearcher.com/news/cornea-sense-secures-over-1-1-million-in-pre-seed-funding-for-ophthalmic-diagnostics)), Synoste (limb-lengthening implant, acquired by Globus Medical, [Aalto](https://aalto.fi/en/innovation-portfolio/synoste)), Symptonic (preparing a 2026 spin-out), Surgify Medical, Otoscreen (est. 2023), VitalSigns (diagnostic tool, status unverified), Maculaser (2019, status unknown).

## Trends
- AI and data-driven preventive health (Business Finland: "human-centric, digital, data-driven").
- Wearables scaling to IPO-level capital; the Oura IPO was reportedly postponed in late September 2026.
- Neuro-stimulation moving from research to certified rehabilitation use (NBS 6 MDR certification).
- Point-of-care diagnostics is an EU priority.
- Aalto's spinout model: 18 spin-offs in just over two years, and 3rd in Europe for spinout funding in H1 2026 ([Aalto](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/aalto-ranks-third-in-europe-for-university-spinout-funding-this-is-the-recipe-for-creating-new)).

## Regulation and policy
- MDR/IVDR targeted revision, COM(2025) 1023 (16 Dec 2025), file 2025/0404(COD). On 1 July 2026 the SANT rapporteur published a draft report with over 130 amendments, and IMCO has a draft opinion ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/product-liability-safety/1827990/how-will-the-eu-parliament-shape-the-mdrivdr-revision-draft-report-indicates-how-parliament-may-seek-to-amend-commissions-proposals)). A Parliament vote is forecast for Q1 2027 and adoption for Q2-Q3 2027 (secondary sources, [Emergo](https://www.emergobyul.com/news/europe-targets-revised-mdr-and-ivdr-adoption-q2-q3-2027?linkId=1018968549)). No Council general approach was found. BioMed Alliance and BEUC have raised safety concerns. MedTech Europe backs the reform but asks for changes.
- AI Act omnibus: provisional deal 7 May 2026. Medical AI stays high-risk, with reported application dates of 2028-08-02 (AI in devices) and 2027-12-02 (standalone). Secondary sources only, so verify against the Official Journal.
- EHDS: implementation is ongoing. The interaction with the AI omnibus is contested by CPME.

## Opportunities for Aalto inventions
- Horizon Europe HLTH 2027 calls (see funding_calls): point-of-care diagnostics IA (2027-09-16), AI biomarkers RIA (2027-09-16), disabilities and independent living RIA (2027-02-17), virtual human twins RIA (2027-09-16), Bridging call for taking Pillar II results to market (2027-01-28).
- Business Finland Health 360 and Research Council of Finland Proof of Concept for early-stage commercialisation.
- Brain technology and neuroimaging, diagnostics hardware (sensors, capsules, ophthalmic devices) and AI-based health analytics fit the Finnish ecosystem and its clinical partners.
- Advisors should plan an MDR route early. The revision may lower burden but is not yet law.
