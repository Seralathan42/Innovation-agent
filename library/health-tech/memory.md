# Health technology: knowledge file (updated 2026-11-16)

## Overview
Scope: medical devices, diagnostics and imaging, digital health and brain technology, with a focus on Europe and Finland. Finland has a strong cluster in:
- wearables: Oura
- brain stimulation: Nexstim, an Aalto-launched company ([Aalto](https://aalto.fi/en/news/aalto-university-launched-brain-stimulation-company-lists-on-the-stock-exchange))
- monitoring: Bittium
- eye diagnostics: Revenio
- blood-based risk detection: Nightingale Health
- dental imaging: Planmeca

Public support is large: Business Finland's Health 360 Finland programme, Research to Business funding, Horizon Europe and the EIC. The Research Council of Finland will not open a Proof of Concept call in 2027, so ERC PoC, R2B and the EIC matter more (see Opportunities).

## Market size and growth
- **European medtech market:** about EUR 173 billion in 2025 (MedTech Europe estimate). Medical devices grew 5.6% per year on average over the past 10 years and IVD 3.6% per year. Europe is about 25.5-25.6% of the world market ([MedTech Europe Datahub, updated 2026-10-06](https://www.medtecheurope.org/datahub/market)). Company and employment counts from the 2026 edition were not readable ([MedTech Europe](https://www.medtecheurope.org/datahub/introduction/)).
- **EU-27, manufacture of medical and dental instruments and supplies (NACE C32.5):**
  - 2024: 62,000 enterprises, about 580,000 persons employed, wages and salaries EUR 23.1 billion.
  - 2023: 63,227 enterprises, 600,000 persons employed.
  - Source: [Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table). EU turnover for this class was not returned.
- **Finland, same class (C32.5), 2024:** 434 enterprises, 2,529 persons employed, net turnover EUR 447.5 million (EUR 381.1 million in 2023, up 17.4%), value added EUR 174.6 million ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)). The class excludes electromedical and imaging equipment (NACE 26.6) and software, so it understates the Finnish sector.
- **Finland trade, 2025** ([Eurostat Comext DS-045409](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/DS-045409/default/table)):
  - Medical instruments (HS 9018): exports EUR 1.487 billion (EUR 1.303 billion in 2024, up 14.1%), imports EUR 654.7 million.
  - X-ray apparatus (HS 9022): exports EUR 295.9 million (flat).
  - Orthopaedic appliances and implants (HS 9021): exports EUR 63.8 million, imports EUR 227.2 million.
- **EU-27 extra-EU trade in HS 9018, 2025:** exports EUR 35.6 billion (up 2.7%), imports EUR 28.3 billion ([Eurostat Comext DS-045409](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/DS-045409/default/table)).
- **Finnish health technology product exports:** record EUR 2.79 billion in 2025 (up 8%), trade surplus EUR 1.2 billion, EUR 0.92 billion to the US (about a third). The figure comes from customs data and excludes software and digital services ([Healthtech Finland](https://teknologiateollisuus.fi/healthtech/terveysteknologia-osana-terveysalaa/vientitilastot/)). The product scope is not listed.

## Trends
- **AI and data-driven preventive health.** Business Finland calls this "human-centric, digital, data-driven". The Commission's 2027 Work Programme (4 Nov 2026) reportedly names AI-enabled healthcare among five priority sectors (secondary, [Nelson Advisors](https://www.healthcare.digital/single-post/nelson-advisors-this-week-in-european-healthtech-medtech-and-health-ai-13th-november-2026)).
- **Wearables moving into care delivery.** Oura plus Counsel Health adds AI primary care to a device business, backed by fast revenue growth.
- **Consolidation in diagnostics and surgical robotics** (Revenio-Visionix, Stryker-CMR). Advisors report that milestone-based earn-outs tied to CE marks and reimbursement are now the norm in European health AI M&A ([Nelson Advisors](https://www.healthcare.digital/single-post/nelson-advisors-this-week-in-european-healthtech-medtech-and-health-ai-13th-november-2026)). This means founders should expect part of the exit value to depend on regulatory milestones.
- **Neuro-stimulation moving from research to certified use.** NBS 6 has MDR certification, new indications such as Alzheimer's are emerging, and BCI interest is growing. Aalto's Slush health line-up is neuro- and eye-heavy.
- **Ophthalmic diagnostics and therapy is a Finnish strength** (Revenio, Maculaser, Cornea Sense, EyeInsight). Strategic investors such as ZEISS Ventures are active.
- **Point-of-care diagnostics is an EU priority.**
- **Research activity.** Magnetoencephalography papers rose from 2,208 (2017) to 4,101 (2026). Aalto is among the ten most active institutions with 683 papers (OpenAlex via the research_trend tool; no public link).
- **Aalto spinout model.** Eight research-based spinouts so far in 2026, with a target of 20 per year. Spinouts raised over EUR 500 million in H1 2026 (mostly ICEYE), ranking Aalto 3rd in Europe ([Aalto, 2026-11-03](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/aalto-ranks-third-in-europe-for-university-spinout-funding-this-is-the-recipe-for-creating-new)).
- **Women's health.** The Eir Accelerator (Turku) received 304 applications from 53 countries. Its first cohort starts at Slush, dated 2026-11-16 or 2026-11-18 depending on the source. Its investor network includes Innovestor, Inventure and Voima Ventures ([Business Turku](https://businessturku.fi/en/finland-bets-on-a-category-the-world-has-underfunded-over-300-founders-answer-the-call-for-womens-health/); [Femtech Insider](https://femtechinsider.com/finland-launches-eir-accelerator-a-dedicated-womens-health-startup-program/)).

## Regulation and policy
- **MDR/IVDR targeted revision:** COM(2025) 1023 (16 Dec 2025), file 2025/0404(COD).
  - On 1 July 2026 the SANT rapporteur (Oliver Schenk) published a draft report with over 130 amendments; nearly 800 amendments were tabled in total ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/product-liability-safety/1827990/how-will-the-eu-parliament-shape-the-mdrivdr-revision-draft-report-indicates-how-parliament-may-seek-to-amend-commissions-proposals); [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=b9af410f-1dfe-4d5b-911d-c442aebf109d)).
  - A SANT committee vote has been reported for 3 Dec 2026 ([DSV, July 2026](https://dsv-europa.de/en/news/2026/07/mdr-ivdr.html)). The Observatory lists an indicative plenary of 14 Dec 2026 as a planning marker only ([DeeMED, 2026-10-05](https://www.deemedconsulting.com/insights/2026-10-05-eu-mdr-ivdr-targeted-revision-proposal-status)). Neither date is confirmed, and other sources expect the Parliament vote in Q1 2027 and adoption in Q2-Q3 2027 ([Emergo](https://www.emergobyul.com/news/europe-targets-revised-mdr-and-ivdr-adoption-q2-q3-2027?linkId=1018968549)).
  - A Parliament hearing with MedTech Europe was held on 10 Nov 2026.
  - No Council general approach has been found.
  - Current MDR/IVDR rules apply until the revision is adopted. BioMed Alliance and BEUC have raised safety concerns ([BioMed Alliance](https://www.biomedeurope.org/news/healthcare-professionals-share-views-as-negotiations-on-mdr-ivdr-revision-continue/)).
- **AI Act omnibus:** reported as Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, published 24 July 2026, in force 27 July 2026. High-risk rules for AI in Annex I products apply from 2028-08-02 and for Annex III systems from 2027-12-02 ([NicFab](https://www.nicfab.eu/en/posts/digital-omnibus-ai-official-journal/)). That AI in medical devices falls under the 2028 date is my reading of Annex I. The Official Journal text has not been read directly.
- **UK:** on 2026-10-21 the government confirmed indefinite acceptance of CE-marked devices and IVDs in Great Britain ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-confirms-indefinite-recognition-of-ce-marked-medical-devices)). No effective date was stated.
- **Switzerland:** Swissmedic is preparing swissdamed registration updates (industry webinar on 10 Nov 2026).
- **EHDS:** implementation is ongoing. CPME contests how it interacts with the AI omnibus.

## Opportunities for Aalto inventions

  - Funds up to EUR 850,000 per project at 50% co-funding, of which EUR 400,000-500,000 goes to the commercialising company.
