# AI and software: sector knowledge file (updated 2026-11-19)

## Overview
Scope: AI, machine learning, data platforms, robotics software and industrial software, with a focus on Europe and Finland. Capital is flowing into AI infrastructure, defence and physical AI, energy-optimisation software and quantum-AI. Growth rounds are large (Helsing $1.8bn in July 2026; Mistral €3bn in September 2026; Agile Robots $500m in November 2026). Finnish firms use AI at about twice the EU rate. EU regulation has eased in the short term. The main Horizon Europe Apply AI, data and robotics calls opened on 2026-11-17 and close 2027-03-18. The EIC 2027 calls are opening in November 2026.

## Market size and growth
Official statistics (queried 2026-11-16, not re-queried this run):
- Enterprise AI use (10+ staff), 2025: Finland 37.82%, EU27 19.95%, Denmark 42.03%, Sweden 35.04%. In 2024: Finland 24.37%, EU27 13.48%. In 2023: Finland 15.1%, EU27 8.06% ([Eurostat isoc_eb_ai](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/isoc_eb_ai/default/table)).
- Finland, computer programming and consultancy (NACE J62), 2024: 14,792 enterprises, €13.1bn net turnover, 69,452 persons employed. In 2023: 14,091 enterprises, €12.6bn, 70,544 employed. Turnover grew about 4% while employment fell slightly ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).
- Finland, software publishing (NACE J58.2), 2024: 302 enterprises, €5.61bn net turnover, 9,775 persons employed (2023: 268 enterprises, €5.11bn) ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).
- EU27, NACE J62, 2024: 1,027,378 enterprises and about €940bn net turnover (2023: 964,677 and about €880bn). The EU turnover figures are rounded in the dataset ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).
- ICT specialists, 2025: Finland 202,300 people, 7.8% of employment; EU27 10.45m, 5.0% ([Eurostat isoc_sks_itspt](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/isoc_sks_itspt/default/table)).
- These NACE figures cover the whole software and IT services industry, not AI alone. Total AI market size: still no reliable figure found. Do not cite one without a source.

Venture funding (secondary sources):

UK $12bn, Germany $3.5bn, France $2.9bn ([HumanX/Crunchbase via Intelligent CIO, 2026-08-17](https://www.intelligentcio.com/eu/2026/08/17/european-ai-start-ups-capture-55-of-vc-funding-as-investment-hits-us23-billion/)).

- AI infrastructure and sovereign compute. The EuroHPC AI Gigafactories call (up to seven sites, up to €10bn public funding, more than €20bn private expected) ran 2026-07-30 to 2026-11-12; selection is due in early 2027 ([StorageNewsletter, 2026-08-18](https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2026/08/18/eu-launches-ai-gigafactories-call-to-boost-europes-computing-capacity-and-unlock-more-than-e30-billion-in-investment/)). No official EuroHPC page was read.
- Inference efficiency and chip-agnostic software (Daisytuner).
- Defence and dual-use physical AI, with very large rounds.
- Robot learning, humanoids and foundation models for robotics (Agile Robots).
- AI plus quantum.
- AI for energy optimisation, with funded Finnish players (Intergrid, Capalo AI, Automous).
- AI governance and compliance tooling (Saidot).
- Capital is concentrated: 73% of H1 2026 AI VC went to 38 large rounds. October 2026 total European funding was reportedly sharply lower than September.
- Vertical AI for industry: construction (Stoa), maintenance, healthcare intake.

## Regulation and policy
- AI Act Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: published 2026-07-24, in force 2026-07-27 ([White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-ai-omnibus-enters-force-amending-ai-act); [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-approach-artificial-intelligence)). It delays high-risk obligations to 2027-12-02 (Annex III) and 2028-08-02 (Annex I). EUR-Lex has not been read directly. New Article 5 bans apply from 2026-12-02. It also amends the Machinery Regulation and the civil aviation regulation.
- Transparency (Article 50): Code of Practice on marking AI-generated content published 2026-06-10; Commission guidelines 2026-07-20; AI Office and national authorities enforce from 2026-08-02 ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-approach-artificial-intelligence)). A claim that pre-August systems have until 2026-12-02 for machine-readable marking is unverified.
- GPAI obligations have applied since 2025-08-02 and are not postponed; older models must comply by 2027-08-02 (secondary sources). High-risk classification guidance is expected by end 2026 (secondary).
- Tech Sovereignty Package, including the Cloud and AI Development Act, proposed 2026-06-03 ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-approach-artificial-intelligence)). The data and ePrivacy omnibus is still in the pipeline.
- Horizon Europe Apply AI and Frontier AI calls opened 2026-11-17 and close 2027-03-18 ([EU Funding & Tenders portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/home)). Most restrict participation to Member States and a short list of associated countries.
- Finland: Business Finland Research to Business call 2/2026 closed 2026-09-16 (€7.5m; decisions January 2027) ([Business Finland](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/r2b-funding-call-2-2026/)). No 1/2027 call page found. RCF Proof of Concept 2026 closed 2026-05-06 (€20m) ([RCF](https://www.aka.fi/en/research-funding/apply-for-funding/calls-for-applications/apply-now2/2026-rcf-proof-of-concept-funding/)); no next call announced. Business Finland's AI call for high-growth companies ended 2026-04-15 ([Business Finland](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/funding-call-ai/)).
- Aalto is scaling its spin-off programme from 0–5 a year to up to 25 a year by 2030 ([Aalto](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/aalto-ranks-third-in-europe-for-university-spinout-funding-this-is-the-recipe-for-creating-new)).
