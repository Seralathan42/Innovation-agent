# AI and software: sector knowledge file (updated 2026-11-10)

## Overview
Scope: AI, machine learning, data platforms, robotics software and industrial software, with a focus on Europe and Finland. Capital is flowing into AI infrastructure, defence and physical AI, and quantum-AI. Growth rounds are large (Helsing closed $1.8bn in July 2026; Mistral €3bn in September 2026). Finnish firms use AI at about twice the EU rate. EU regulation has eased in the short term. A large set of Horizon Europe Apply AI and robotics calls opens on 2026-11-17.

## Market size and growth
- Enterprise AI use (10+ staff): Finland 37.82%, EU27 19.95% in 2025 ([Eurostat isoc_eb_ai](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/isoc_eb_ai/default/table)). Carried over from the first run and not re-queried.
- European AI VC H1 2026: US$23bn, up 130% from US$10bn in H1 2025, 55% of all European VC. 73% went to 38 companies with rounds of $100m or more. UK $12bn, Germany $3.5bn, France $2.9bn ([HumanX/Crunchbase via Intelligent CIO, 2026-08-17](https://www.intelligentcio.com/eu/2026/08/17/european-ai-start-ups-capture-55-of-vc-funding-as-investment-hits-us23-billion/)).
- A narrower count: tech.eu puts H1 2026 at about €6.0bn across 156 rounds (149 companies) ([funding.tech.eu](https://funding.tech.eu/sectors/ai/2026/h1)). The two sources differ in methodology, so do not mix them.
- Europe Q3 2026 VC: €13.7bn in 1,300+ rounds ([dig.watch](https://dig.watch)); carried over and not re-verified.
- Finnish tech funding H1 2026: about €1.3bn in 53 deals. ICEYE (space) was €900m of that, so AI is a smaller share ([tech.eu, 2026-09-17](https://tech.eu)).
- October 2025 reference: 27 European AI rounds worth €446m ([funding.tech.eu](https://funding.tech.eu/sectors/ai/2025/10)). No October 2026 total was found.
- Total AI market size: no reliable figure found. Do not cite one without a source.

## Trends
- AI infrastructure and sovereign compute (EuroHPC AI Gigafactories tender for up to seven sites, reportedly closing 2026-11-12, [source](https://theaiinsider.tech/?p=50148); unverified).
- Defence and dual-use physical AI, with very large rounds.
- Robot learning and foundation models for robotics (Machenta).
- AI plus quantum.
- AI for energy optimisation.
- AI governance and compliance tooling (Saidot, with a policy-as-code focus).
- Growth-stage rounds are returning, but capital is concentrated: 73% of H1 2026 AI VC went to 38 large rounds.
- Vertical AI for industry, such as construction materials and maintenance.

## Regulation and policy
- AI Act Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: published in the Official Journal 2026-07-24, in force 2026-07-27 ([White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-ai-omnibus-enters-force-amending-ai-act)). It delays high-risk obligations to December 2027 (Annex III) and August 2028 (Annex I). The exact dates (2027-12-02 and 2028-08-02) still need checking on EUR-Lex. New Article 5 bans on non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material AI apply from 2026-12-02. It also amends the Machinery Regulation and the civil aviation regulation. Sources disagree on GPAI date details, so check EUR-Lex. The wider data and ePrivacy omnibus is still in the legislative pipeline.
- Horizon Europe "Apply AI" and Frontier AI Initiative calls open 2026-11-17 and close 2027-03-18 ([EU funding calls tool](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/home)).
- Aalto is scaling its spin-off programme from 0–5 a year to up to 25 a year by 2030 ([Aalto](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/aalto-ranks-third-in-europe-for-university-spinout-funding-this-is-the-recipe-for-creating-new)).

## Opportunities for Aalto inventions
- AI-driven robotics for industry (HORIZON-CL4-2027-04-DIGITAL-EMERGING-05, deadline 2027-03-18) and trustworthy generative AI for robotics (DIGITAL-EMERGING-07, deadline 2027-02-10). Both fit Aalto robotics and automation research. Also open: Industrial leadership in AI, Data and Robotics (DIGITAL-EMERGING-01-04, deadline 2027-01-27).
- Federated and energy-efficient AI (DATA-03 and DATA-09, deadline 2027-03-18).
- Frontier AI and challenge-driven AI (DIGITAL-EMERGING-11 and -04, deadline 2027-03-18); Human/AI collaboration (DIGITAL-EMERGING-52, deadline 2027-09-02).
- Apply AI energy platforms pilot (HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-01-12, deadline 2027-02-16).
- Automated scientific discovery in materials (RAISE pilot, deadline 2027-02-02).
- Pillar II results to market (HORIZON-BRIDGING-2027-02-05, deadline 2027-01-28), a commercialisation route.
- AI governance and compliance tools, a market served by Saidot, now with fresh capital.
- Industrial maintenance AI, following the Rotomate model.
- Defence and dual-use AI, where Tesi, Nokia and Business Finland are active.
- Cybersecurity AI (DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-CYBERAI, deadline 2027-01-14; HORIZON-CL3-2027-02-CS-ECCC-01, deadline 2027-09-15).
- Aalto AI spin-offs: RealYou AI (pre-business) is the one confirmed AI example. Automous Oy and Deltan Labs are adjacent (energy and materials optimisation).
