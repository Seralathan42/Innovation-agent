# AI and software: sector knowledge file (updated 2026-11-16)

## Overview
Scope: AI, machine learning, data platforms, robotics software and industrial software, with a focus on Europe and Finland. Capital is flowing into AI infrastructure, defence and physical AI, energy-optimisation software and quantum-AI. Growth rounds are large (Helsing closed $1.8bn in July 2026; Mistral €3bn in September 2026; Agile Robots $500m in November 2026). Finnish firms use AI at about twice the EU rate. EU regulation has eased in the short term. The main Horizon Europe Apply AI, data and robotics calls open on 2026-11-17, and budgets are now known (€18m to €44m per topic). The EIC 2027 calls are opening in November 2026.

## Market size and growth
Official statistics (queried 2026-11-16):
- Enterprise AI use (10+ staff), 2025: Finland 37.82%, EU27 19.95%, Denmark 42.03%, Sweden 35.04%. In 2024: Finland 24.37%, EU27 13.48%. In 2023: Finland 15.1%, EU27 8.06% ([Eurostat isoc_eb_ai](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/isoc_eb_ai/default/table)). Re-queried this run and unchanged.
- Finland, computer programming and consultancy (NACE J62), 2024: 14,792 enterprises, €13.1bn net turnover, 69,452 persons employed. In 2023: 14,091 enterprises, €12.6bn, 70,544 employed. Turnover grew about 4% while employment fell slightly ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).
- Finland, software publishing (NACE J58.2), 2024: 302 enterprises, €5.61bn net turnover, 9,775 persons employed (2023: 268 enterprises, €5.11bn) ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).
- EU27, NACE J62, 2024: 1,027,378 enterprises and about €940bn net turnover (2023: 964,677 and about €880bn). The EU turnover figures are rounded in the dataset ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).
- ICT specialists, 2025: Finland 202,300 people, 7.8% of employment; EU27 10.45m, 5.0% ([Eurostat isoc_sks_itspt](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/isoc_sks_itspt/default/table)).
- These NACE figures cover the whole software and IT services industry, not AI alone. Total AI market size: still no reliable figure found. Do not cite one without a source.

Venture funding (secondary sources):
- European AI VC H1 2026: US$23bn, up 130% from US$10bn in H1 2025, 55% of all European VC. 73% went to 38 companies with rounds of $100m or more. UK $12bn, Germany $3.5bn, France $2.9bn ([HumanX/Crunchbase via Intelligent CIO, 2026-08-17](https://www.intelligentcio.com/eu/2026/08/17/european-ai-start-ups-capture-55-of-vc-funding-as-investment-hits-us23-billion/)).

- October 2026, all European startup funding: reported at €6bn across 288 rounds, down 62% from €15.8bn in September ([EuropeStartupJobs daily, 2026-11-12](https://europestartupjobs.com/europe-startup-daily-2026-11-12/)). Single aggregator, all sectors, indicative only. No AI-only October 2026 total was found.

- Aalto spinouts raised over €500m in H1 2026 (third in Europe after Oxford at over €900m and Cambridge), mostly ICEYE; about €2.6bn since 2010; eight research-based spinouts launched so far in 2026 ([Aalto](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/aalto-ranks-third-in-europe-for-university-spinout-funding-this-is-the-recipe-for-creating-new); figures as relayed by [ATHENA, 2026-11-06](https://grantoffice.athenauni.eu/2026/11/06/aalto-ranks-third-in-europe-for-university-spinout-funding-this-is-the-recipe-for-creating-new-industries/)).

## Trends
- AI infrastructure and sovereign compute. The EuroHPC AI Gigafactories call (up to seven sites, up to €10bn public funding, more than €20bn private expected) opened 2026-07-30 and closed 2026-11-12; selection is due in early 2027 and sites should operate within 18 months of selection ([StorageNewsletter, 2026-08-18](https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2026/08/18/eu-launches-ai-gigafactories-call-to-boost-europes-computing-capacity-and-unlock-more-than-e30-billion-in-investment/)). Dates come from secondary sources that agree with each other; no official EuroHPC page was read.
- Inference efficiency and chip-agnostic software (Daisytuner), in line with the EU call on energy-efficient AI data processing.
- Defence and dual-use physical AI, with very large rounds.
- Robot learning, humanoids and foundation models for robotics (Agile Robots, Machenta).
- AI plus quantum.
- AI for energy optimisation, now with three funded Finnish players (Intergrid, Capalo AI, Automous).
- AI governance and compliance tooling (Saidot, with a policy-as-code focus).
- Growth-stage rounds are returning, but capital is concentrated: 73% of H1 2026 AI VC went to 38 large rounds. October 2026 total European funding was reportedly sharply lower than September.
- Vertical AI for industry, such as construction materials, maintenance and healthcare intake.

## Regulation and policy
- AI Act Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744: published in the Official Journal 2026-07-24, in force 2026-07-27 ([White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/eu-ai-omnibus-enters-force-amending-ai-act); the Commission's AI page confirms the 27 July date, [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-approach-artificial-intelligence)). It delays high-risk obligations to 2 December 2027 (Annex III) and 2 August 2028 (Annex I). These two dates are now repeated by several secondary sources citing the Commission timeline, but EUR-Lex itself has still not been read. New Article 5 bans on non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material AI apply from 2026-12-02. It also amends the Machinery Regulation and the civil aviation regulation.
- Transparency (Article 50): Code of Practice on marking and labelling AI-generated content published 2026-06-10; Commission guidelines published 2026-07-20; AI Office and national authorities enforce the AI Act from 2026-08-02 ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-approach-artificial-intelligence)). One secondary source says generative AI systems already on the market before 2 August 2026 have until 2026-12-02 to add machine-readable marking; not verified. A report of a further Article 50 communication on 2026-11-05 could not be confirmed.
- GPAI obligations have applied since 2025-08-02 and are not postponed; models placed on the market before that date must comply by 2027-08-02 (secondary sources). High-risk classification guidance is expected by the end of 2026 (secondary sources).
- Tech Sovereignty Package, including the Cloud and AI Development Act, proposed 2026-06-03 ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-approach-artificial-intelligence)). The wider data and ePrivacy omnibus is still in the legislative pipeline; nothing new found.
- Horizon Europe Apply AI and Frontier AI Initiative calls open 2026-11-17 and close 2027-03-18 ([EU Funding & Tenders portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/home)). Most restrict participation to EU Member States, Iceland, Norway and a short list of associated countries.
- Finland: Business Finland Research to Business call 2/2026 closed 2026-09-16 (€7.5m, 80% grant, projects of €400,000 to €700,000, decisions due January 2027) ([Business Finland](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/r2b-funding-call-2-2026/)). No 1/2027 call page exists yet; calls usually run in spring and autumn. Research Council of Finland Proof of Concept 2026 closed 2026-05-06 (€20m; up to €300,000 per project, €450,000 per consortium) ([RCF](https://www.aka.fi/en/research-funding/apply-for-funding/calls-for-applications/apply-now2/2026-rcf-proof-of-concept-funding/)); no next call announced. Business Finland's AI call for high-growth companies ended 2026-04-15 ([Business Finland](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/funding-call-ai/)).
- Aalto is scaling its spin-off programme from 0–5 a year to up to 25 a year by 2030 ([Aalto](https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/aalto-ranks-third-in-europe-for-university-spinout-funding-this-is-the-recipe-for-creating-new)); eight had launched in 2026 by early November.
