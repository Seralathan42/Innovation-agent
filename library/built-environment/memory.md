# Built environment: knowledge library
Last updated: 2026-11-25

## Overview
Scope: construction technology, smart buildings, urban mobility and water systems, with a focus on Europe and Finland.

The sector is cyclical. Finnish and EU construction fell in 2023-24 and recovered in 2025. In 2026 the two have diverged:
- Finnish output is growing at double-digit rates (+12.6% year on year in September 2026), driven by data centres and specialised trades ([Eurostat sts_copr_m](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_copr_m/default/table)). Statistics Finland's turnover index confirms this: +13.2% in September ([Statistics Finland, 10 Nov 2026](https://stat.fi/fi/julkaisu/cmg0qmciwaxrs07uptm8o6hsb)).
- EU27 output is slightly negative (August -0.5%, September -1.4%), and EU building construction is still shrinking.

Finnish housing construction remains at a historic low ([RT, Oct 2026](https://rt.fi/tiedotteet-ja-uutiset/2026/10/datakeskukset-ovat-vetaneet-rakentamisen-kasvuun/)). Google's at-least-EUR 13 billion Finnish data-centre programme for 2027-2028 (Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos, Vaala) supports the pipeline ([DCD, Sept 2026](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-pledges-13bn-investment-in-digital-infrastructure-in-finland/)). Yle counts 145 announced Finnish data-centre projects, 60 of them in 2026 ([Yle](https://yle.fi/a/74-20250264)).

Construction tech (AI, robotics, site data) is seeing strong VC activity, from Nordic pre-seed rounds (Stoa, EUR 3.5M) to $600M late-stage rounds. The EU policy push favours renovation, circularity, LCA, energy-efficient buildings and digital water: EPBD, New European Bauhaus, the Water Resilience Strategy and its digitalisation action plan, and the Affordable Housing Act.

## Market size and growth
**Structural size**
- Finland, construction (NACE F), 2024: 57,350 enterprises, 169,006 persons employed, net turnover EUR 41.0 billion. EU27: 4.07 million enterprises, 13.9 million persons employed, net turnover EUR 2,312 billion ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).
- Finland, water sector, 2024 ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)). This is the utility market, not the water-technology vendor market.
  - Water supply (E36): 1,328 enterprises, 3,395 persons employed, turnover EUR 1,279M.
  - Sewerage (E37): 343 enterprises, 1,663 persons employed, turnover EUR 437M.
  - Together about EUR 1.72 billion. EU27: water supply EUR 62.7 billion, sewerage EUR 30.0 billion.
- EU water sector value added: EUR 111.7 billion, with 1.6 million jobs ([European Commission, 9 Nov 2026](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/commissioner-roswall-discusses-action-plan-digitalise-european-water-sector-2026-11-09_en)).
- Finnish renovation construction was worth EUR 14.9 billion in 2025 and is now a larger share of construction value than new build ([RT, Oct 2026](https://rt.fi/tiedotteet-ja-uutiset/2026/10/datakeskukset-ovat-vetaneet-rakentamisen-kasvuun/)).

**Annual production** ([Eurostat sts_copr_a](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_copr_a/default/table))
- EU27 construction: +2.3% in 2025 (index 105.1, 2021=100), after -1.8% in 2024 and +2.1% in 2023.
- EU27 construction of buildings is weak: index 96.9 in 2025 (-0.2%), after -6.6% in 2024. Civil engineering is strong: index 115.4 (+2.2% in 2025).
- Finland construction: +6.1% in 2025 (index 97.5), after -7.6% in 2023 and -1.7% in 2024. In 2025, buildings rose 4.3% but the index is only 83.0, specialised construction rose 8.9%, and civil engineering fell 1.9%.

## Trends
- **Data centres** are the main driver of Finnish non-residential building and shape incumbents' strategy: YIT's EUR 1 billion 2029 ambition, contracts for SRV, Skanska and NCC, and Google's EUR 13 billion programme. Permits confirm the pipeline (non-residential +37%). Construction-tech startups follow: Buildots and Stoa both target data-centre projects.
  - Risks: power availability (YIT's stated condition); grid congestion pushing projects north, with possible rules steering large power users to remote areas ([Yle](https://yle.fi/a/74-20250264)); a reported pause of two Google builds over forest clearing (secondary source, unverified); US local backlash (40 of 41 ballot measures on 3 Nov 2026 favoured restrictions, per [ConTech Roundup](https://contechroundup.substack.com/p/last-week-in-contech-9-november-2026)).
- **Renovation** is larger than new build in Finland by value, but RT expects stronger renovation growth only in 2027. Specialised construction is the fastest-growing segment in 2026.
- **Incumbent margins:** incumbents cut costs earlier in 2026 and now report margin improvement and record backlogs (Skanska).
- **VC** money is going to AI and robotics for design, planning, procurement and site information. Rounds are getting larger at the top, and Nordic angel and seed networks now back construction AI (Stoa). Corporate-backed specialist funds are growing (Zacua Fund II).
- **Water digitalisation:**
  - The Commission's action plan is due in the first half of 2027 ([European Commission](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/commissioner-roswall-discusses-action-plan-digitalise-european-water-sector-2026-11-09_en)).
  - The Commission cites up to 25% savings from smart meters and 7-14% from leak detection ([Smart Water Magazine](https://smartwatermagazine.com/news/smart-water-magazine/eu-water-digitalisation-plan-takes-shape-as-operators-and-industry-set-out-their-priorities)).
  - EIT Water's first calls are reportedly expected in 2027 ([Smart Water Utilities](https://www.smart-water-utilities.com/news/europe-launches-sweeping-water-innovation-drive)).
- **Horizon topics:** heat recovery from IT rooms, thermal comfort, on-site robotics, circular LCA, predictive maintenance, hybrid materials, healthier construction sites.
- **Aalto spin-outs:** Aalto reported ranking third in Europe for spin-out funding in the first half of 2026 ([Aalto news, Swedish](https://www.aalto.fi/sv/nyheter/aalto-tredje-i-europa-i-finansiering-av-universitets-spinouts-sa-formades-receptet-for-att-skapa-nya)). Needs checking.
