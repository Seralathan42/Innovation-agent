# Built environment: knowledge library
Last updated: 2026-11-09

## Overview
Scope: construction technology, smart buildings, urban mobility and water systems, with a focus on Europe and Finland. The sector is cyclical. Finnish and EU construction fell in 2023-24 and recovered in 2025. Construction tech (AI, robotics, site data) is seeing strong VC activity, from seed to $130M late-stage rounds in September 2026. The EU policy push (EPBD, New European Bauhaus, Water Resilience Strategy) favours renovation, circularity, LCA and energy-efficient buildings.

## Market size and growth
- EU27 construction production +2.3% in 2025 (index 105.1, 2021=100), after -1.8% in 2024 and +2.1% in 2023. Source: [Eurostat sts_copr_a](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_copr_a/default/table).
- EU27 construction of buildings is weak: index 96.9 in 2025 (-0.2%), after -6.6% in 2024. Civil engineering is strong: index 115.4 (+2.2% in 2025). Source: [Eurostat sts_copr_a](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_copr_a/default/table).
- Finland construction +6.1% in 2025 (index 97.5), after -7.6% (2023) and -1.7% (2024). Buildings +4.3% in 2025 but index only 83.0. Specialised construction (renovation and installation trades) +9.0%. Source: [Eurostat sts_copr_a](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_copr_a/default/table).
- Finnish building permits: 12-month volume about 37M m3, +19% year on year in August 2026. Non-residential-led (residential -7%, other buildings +15% in March 2026). Source: [Statistics Finland, 2026](https://stat.fi/en/publication/cmfxlb8z094ck07w33f2j03gr).
- Forecasts: ETLA expects the downturn to end between 2025 and 2027. B+L says Finland's residential recovery is not sustainable before 2027. A paid ResearchAndMarkets report projects 3.4% average growth for 2026-2029 (low reliability). These were carried over from the first run and not re-checked.
- Not found: reliable euro market sizes for Finnish smart buildings, urban mobility or water tech, and a Finnish construction-tech company count. Do not quote numbers for these until sourced.
- Not found in this run: YIT, Skanska, Kone and Caverion Q3 2026 results (searches returned only older material). The only YIT figures found were Q1 2026 (revenue about EUR 90M, adjusted operating profit EUR 13M, per a transcript summary of uncertain scope), which I would not quote.

## Trends
- Data centres are a main driver of Finnish non-residential building (Skanska EUR 77M and EUR 100M, YIT/atNorth Kouvola). Buildots also cites data centres and industrial sites as its expansion target.
- Incumbents are cutting costs (YIT restructuring, April 2026).
- VC money is going to AI and robotics for design, planning, procurement and site work, and rounds are getting larger (Buildots $130M, Miter $40M). About 84% of surveyed investors plan to maintain or increase construction-tech funding in 2026 (Zacua Ventures survey, via ccstartup.com; carried over).
- Heat recovery from IT rooms, thermal comfort and renovation robotics are Horizon topics.
- Aalto reported ranking third in Europe for spin-out funding in the first half of 2026 ([Aalto news, Swedish](https://www.aalto.fi/sv/nyheter/aalto-tredje-i-europa-i-finansiering-av-universitets-spinouts-sa-formades-receptet-for-att-skapa-nya)). The same page says the "Aalto Startup Center" name is being retired. Both points come from a translated summary and need checking.

## Regulation and policy
- EPBD recast: the transposition deadline was 29 May 2026. Finland missed it ([RT, May 2026](https://rt.fi/en/blogi/2026/05/rakennusten-energiatehokkuusvaatimukset-kiristyvat-vaikkakin-myohassa-aikataulustaan/); [REHVA Journal](https://www.rehva.eu/rehva-journal/chapter/implementation-of-epbd-still-ongoing-in-finland)). Implementation is split across about five statutes (three government proposals and two decrees were sent for consultation by the Environment Ministry), including a new Act on the Energy Efficiency of Buildings with E-value maximums for offices, commercial and education buildings and hospitals, and solar-energy obligations phased over 2026-2030 ([Government, consultation](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/-/1410903/ymparistoministerio-pyytaa-lausuntoja-rakennusten-energiatehokkuusdirektiivia-toimeenpanevista-saadoksista)). DLA Piper (March 2026) says the Building Act is being amended to implement EPBD and RED II ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/publications/2026/03/finland-top-5-significant-legislative-changes-regulatory-developments-or-trends-affecting)). I found no source confirming entry into force as of November 2026.
- Life-cycle (carbon) analysis: EU requirements start with large new buildings in 2028 and cover all new buildings from 2030. A 2025 Betoni article said Finnish carbon-footprint calculation would start for permits applied from 2026. This may be superseded.
- EU water: the European Water Resilience Strategy is behind a 2027 Horizon open topic.

## Opportunities for Aalto inventions
- Open NEB calls (deadline 2026-12-01): thermal comfort (EUR 12M, about 3 grants of EUR 4M, TRL 5-7, needs demonstration in two climate zones in different countries, incl. one heritage building), repair and maintenance, vertical space, and reuse of vacant spaces. The wood-in-construction call closes 2026-12-15. These suit consortia, so a spin-off would join as a technology partner. Only about three weeks remain, so only teams with an existing consortium are realistic.
- The Horizon AI, Data and Robotics call opened 2026-11-04 (deadline 2027-01-27), which is a possible route for construction-data and robotics inventions.
- 2027 calls: on-site robotics (EUR 20.25M, about 3 grants; must include at least one 3D-printing technique and three demonstrations; prefab-only solutions are out of scope; deadline 2027-09-15), LCA and circularity modelling, modular offsite manufacturing (NEB, 2027-12-01), Water Resilience Strategy (2027-09-14), wastewater valorisation (2027-09-22), Water4All (2027-04-06), DUT (2027-04-15), EIT Urban Mobility (2027-06-03), multimodal hubs (2027-10-07).
- Fit with Aalto strengths: smart building services engineering, digital water (DIWA), building and urban research, water and environmental engineering (Water Lab spin-off NPHarvest shows a path).
- Data-centre heat recovery and building-energy analytics suit the Finnish market.
- Spin-offs should target AI/data tools and LCA/carbon calculation, given the 2028-2030 regulation timeline, and pair with Finnish contractors and service companies for pilots. Complink's procurement niche shows AI-for-construction is a viable student-startup route.
