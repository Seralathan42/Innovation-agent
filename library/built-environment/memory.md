# Built environment: knowledge library
Last updated: 2026-11-16

## Overview
Scope: construction technology, smart buildings, urban mobility and water systems, with a focus on Europe and Finland.

The sector is cyclical. Finnish and EU construction fell in 2023-24 and recovered in 2025. In 2026 the two have diverged:
- Finnish output is growing at double-digit rates (+12.6% year on year in September 2026), driven by data centres and specialised trades ([Eurostat sts_copr_m](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_copr_m/default/table)). Statistics Finland's turnover index confirms this: +13.2% in September ([Statistics Finland, 10 Nov 2026](https://stat.fi/fi/julkaisu/cmg0qmciwaxrs07uptm8o6hsb)).
- EU27 output is roughly flat, and EU building construction is still shrinking.

Finnish housing construction remains at a historic low ([RT, Oct 2026](https://rt.fi/tiedotteet-ja-uutiset/2026/10/datakeskukset-ovat-vetaneet-rakentamisen-kasvuun/)). Google's at-least-EUR 13 billion Finnish data-centre programme for 2027-2028 (Hamina, Kajaani, Muhos, Vaala) supports the data-centre pipeline ([DCD, Sept 2026](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-pledges-13bn-investment-in-digital-infrastructure-in-finland/)).

Construction tech (AI, robotics, site data) is seeing strong VC activity, from Nordic pre-seed rounds (Stoa, EUR 3.5M) to $600M late-stage rounds. The EU policy push favours renovation, circularity, LCA, energy-efficient buildings and digital water: EPBD, New European Bauhaus, the Water Resilience Strategy and its digitalisation action plan, and the Affordable Housing Act.

EU27: 4.07 million enterprises, 13.9 million persons employed, net turnover EUR 2,312 billion ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)).
- Finland, water sector, 2024 ([Eurostat sbs_ovw_act](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sbs_ovw_act/default/table)). This is the utility market, not the water-technology vendor market.
  - Water supply (E36): 1,328 enterprises, 3,395 persons employed, turnover EUR 1,279M.
  - Sewerage (E37): 343 enterprises, 1,663 persons employed, turnover EUR 437M.
  - Together about EUR 1.72 billion. EU27: water supply EUR 62.7 billion, sewerage EUR 30.0 billion.

- Finnish renovation construction was worth EUR 14.9 billion in 2025 and is now a larger share of construction value than new build ([RT, Oct 2026](https://rt.fi/tiedotteet-ja-uutiset/2026/10/datakeskukset-ovat-vetaneet-rakentamisen-kasvuun/)).

**Annual production** ([Eurostat sts_copr_a](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/sts_copr_a/default/table))
- EU27 construction: +2.3% in 2025 (index 105.1, 2021=100), after -1.8% in 2024 and +2.1% in 2023.
- EU27 construction of buildings is weak: index 96.9 in 2025 (-0.2%), after -6.6% in 2024. Civil engineering is strong: index 115.4 (+2.2% in 2025).
- Finland construction: +6.1% in 2025 (index 97.5), after -7.6% in 2023 and -1.7% in 2024. In 2025, buildings rose 4.3% but the index is only 83.0, specialised construction rose 8.9%, and civil engineering fell 1.9%.

## Trends
- **Data centres** are the main driver of Finnish non-residential building and shape incumbents' strategy: YIT's EUR 1 billion 2029 ambition, contracts for SRV, Skanska and NCC, and Google's EUR 13 billion programme. Construction-tech startups are following: Buildots and Stoa both target data-centre projects.
  - Risks: power availability (YIT's stated condition); US local backlash (40 of 41 ballot measures on 3 Nov 2026 favoured restrictions, per [ConTech Roundup](https://contechroundup.substack.com/p/last-week-in-contech-9-november-2026)).
- **Renovation** is larger than new build in Finland by value, but RT expects stronger renovation growth only in 2027 and says financing limits it. Specialised construction is nonetheless the fastest-growing segment in 2026 (+19.8% volume, +20.7% turnover in September).
- **Incumbent margins:** incumbents cut costs earlier in 2026 (YIT restructuring, April) and are now reporting margin improvement and record backlogs (Skanska).
- **VC** money is going to AI and robotics for design, planning, procurement and site information. Rounds are getting larger at the top (Bedrock $600M), and Nordic angel and seed networks (ex-Supercell, Wolt, Nokia people) are now backing construction AI (Stoa). Corporate-backed specialist funds are growing (Zacua Fund II $135M). About 84% of surveyed investors planned to maintain or increase construction-tech funding in 2026 (Zacua survey, via ccstartup.com; carried over).
- **Water digitalisation:**
  - The Commission's action plan (a flagship of the Water Resilience Strategy, linked to a smart-metering-for-all initiative) is due in the first half of 2027. It targets fragmented data, poor interoperability, lack of digital skills and outdated procurement ([European Commission](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/commissioner-roswall-discusses-action-plan-digitalise-european-water-sector-2026-11-09_en)).
  - The Commission cites up to 25% savings from smart meters and 7-14% from leak detection ([Smart Water Magazine](https://smartwatermagazine.com/news/smart-water-magazine/eu-water-digitalisation-plan-takes-shape-as-operators-and-industry-set-out-their-priorities)).
  - EIT Water's first calls are reportedly expected in 2027 ([Smart Water Utilities](https://www.smart-water-utilities.com/news/europe-launches-sweeping-water-innovation-drive)).
- **Horizon topics:** heat recovery from IT rooms, thermal comfort, on-site robotics, circular LCA, predictive maintenance, hybrid conventional and innovative materials, and healthier construction sites.
- **Aalto spin-outs:** Aalto reported ranking third in Europe for spin-out funding in the first half of 2026 ([Aalto news, Swedish](https://www.aalto.fi/sv/nyheter/aalto-tredje-i-europa-i-finansiering-av-universitets-spinouts-sa-formades-receptet-for-att-skapa-nya)). The "Aalto Startup Center" name is reportedly being retired. Both points still need checking.
