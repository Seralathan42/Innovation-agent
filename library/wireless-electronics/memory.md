# Wireless and electronics: knowledge file (updated 2026-11-09, second run)

## Overview
Scope: 6G and mobile communications, semiconductors, photonics, sensors and electronics, with a focus on Europe and Finland. Finland has a strong base: Nokia, the 6G Flagship at Oulu, VTT, Aalto, Tampere and a growing photonics and sensor startup scene. Eurostat queries were again too large to use, so there are still no Eurostat figures. Patent data were not collected.

## Market size and growth
- No reliable sector-wide market size has been gathered; do not quote one.
- Nokia Q3 2026: net sales €5,154M (+10%). Network Infrastructure +15% in constant currency (IP Networks +43%, Optical +18%). AI and cloud customer sales +123%. 2026 comparable operating profit outlook €2.1–2.6B. [Nokia, 2026](https://www.nokia.com/newsroom/nokia-corporation-interim-report-for-q3-2026/)
- EU 6G funding: SNS JU funded five projects worth about €22M (October 2026) [SNS JU, 2026](https://smart-networks.europa.eu/e22-million-to-turn-europes-6g-research-into-industrial-innovation/). A call of about €220M is reported for January 2027 and is not yet in the EU call database [Techzine, 2026](https://www.techzine.eu/news/infrastructure/145223/europe-aims-to-increase-its-influence-on-6g-with-e220-million/).
- Finnish deep-tech rounds: Vexlum €10M in February 2026 (€6M equity led by Kvanted with Tesi and the EIC Fund, €2.4M EIC Accelerator grant, €1.6M Nordea loan) [EU-Startups, 2026](https://www.eu-startups.com/2026/02/vexlum-raises-e10-million-to-scale-semiconductor-laser-manufacturing-for-quantum-and-space-applications/); Agate Sensors €5.6M in 2025 (€4M seed led by Voima Ventures and LIFTT, €1.6M Business Finland grants) [ArcticStartup, 2025](https://arcticstartup.com/agate-sensors-raises-e5-6-million/).
- Finnish startups (all sectors) raised $1.9B across 110 rounds in the year to 23 September 2026. This is a teaser figure, not sector-specific. [Dealroom, 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/169137-finland-launches-three-growth-projects-to-boost-space-chips-and-factory/)
- Trade register: a search for TOL 26110 (electronic components) in Espoo returned 28 companies, including active and ceased ones. This is not a sector-wide count. [PRH/YTJ via finnish_companies tool, 2026]

- Finnish photonics, sensors and semiconductors: Vexlum, Agate Sensors (Aalto spin-out, registered April 2024), Emberion, Comptek Solutions, Detection Technology, Aspocomp. QID Oy (Helsinki, registered 2023) is in the register, but its business is unverified. Photoncycle is unverified.
- Foreign chip firms with Espoo branches in the register: Infineon Technologies Nordic, Synopsys Technology Finland.
- Nordic: Polar Light Technologies (microLED, SE).
- Research: Aalto, University of Oulu (6G Flagship), Tampere University, VTT, 6G Finland ecosystem.

## Trends
- 6G commercial launch now expected around 2029, earlier than the planned 2030–31 (Helsinki summit, 3 November 2026; not rechecked this run).
- Non-terrestrial (satellite) networks are becoming part of the 6G radio access.
- AI and cloud demand is driving optical and IP network sales.
- Early 6G standards work runs through 2026–27. A Standards, Patents and Competitiveness Forum is scheduled for 18 November 2026.
- 6G is linking to defence and resilience: Finland and Sweden launched the joint 6G-FISRE programme (June 2026, per 6G Flagship), and Nokia expanded 6G and defence network R&D in Finland with Business Finland support (17 June 2026, reported in search results; the Nokia page could not be opened) [Nokia, 2026](https://www.nokia.com/nokia-expands-6g-and-defense-network-rd-in-finland-with-support-from-business-finland/). A government grant of €4.8M for an Oulu 5G/6G project was also reported, undated [Finnish Government](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/en/-/236553176/minister-of-defence-hakkanen-oulu-5g-6g-project-to-receive-government-grant-of-eur-4.8-million).
- Photonics and sensing startups are getting seed funding in Finland.
- Business Finland launched growth projects on space, semiconductors and photonics, and AI in manufacturing in early November 2026. They are coordination and market-access vehicles (partner search, EU cooperation, pilot line access), not direct funding. [Dealroom, 2026](https://dealroom.co/news/169137-finland-launches-three-growth-projects-to-boost-space-chips-and-factory/)
- Passive 6G radio-environment concepts such as Aalto's metacrystal panels.

## Regulation and policy
- Chips Act 2.0 was proposed by the Commission on 3 June 2026 (procedure 2026/0139(COD)). It covers strategic projects, demand accelerators, pilot lines, a Region of Excellence label and a preference for procuring startup chips. It has no new headline budget. [AENEAS, 2026](https://aeneas-office.org/2026/06/03/european-commission-unveils-proposed-chips-act-2-0/)
- Status: the Council held a policy debate at the Competitiveness Council (Council agenda says 24 September 2026; a SEMI brief says 24 October, so the date is unclear). Member States are split on budget, technology priorities, EU preference and advanced manufacturing. ITRE has reportedly appointed MEP Oliver Schenk as rapporteur. No Council general approach or Parliament position was found, and trilogues have not started. [SEMI Europe, 2026](https://www.semi.org/sites/semi.org/files/2026-09/SEMI_Europe_July-August_EU_Policy_Brief.pdf)
- Spectrum: a regulatory opinion says 6G could launch by 2030 in already harmonised bands, mainly upper 6 GHz (about ten months old). [Telecoms.com](https://www.telecoms.com/5g-6g/eu-says-there-is-already-enough-spectrum-to-launch-6g-by-2030)
- SNS JU: the final call under Horizon Europe is reported for January 2027.
- Finland's Ministry of Transport and Communications published its 6G roadmap on 17 June 2025 (per search summary, not verified on the primary page).

## Opportunities for Aalto inventions
- Open calls (budgets not retrieved): AI chip demonstrators (DIGITAL-JU-CHIPS-2026-AI1-SG, 2026-12-01), EU–Japan chip collaboration (2026-12-08), Open Internet Stack (2027-01-03), Smart IoT Platforms and decentralized intelligence (2027-01-14), Horizon Bridging (2027-01-28), photonics RIA (2027-03-18), Virtual Worlds hardware (2027-03-18), Telco Edge Cloud 3C pilots (2027-03-18), and MSCA Doctoral Networks with industrial doctorates (2026-11-24).
- The SNS JU 6G call expected in January 2027 is not yet in the EU call database. Watch for it.
- Passive 6G surfaces, sensing, photonic integrated devices and low-power chips fit Aalto research. The Agate Sensors path (spin-out, then seed funding plus Business Finland grants) is a model; Vexlum shows the EIC Accelerator plus Tesi route.
- Chips Act 2.0 procurement preference for startup chips could help chip-design spin-offs.
- The new Business Finland semiconductor and photonics growth project may help spin-offs find partners and pilot lines.
- 6G for defence and resilience is a growing funded theme in Finland.
