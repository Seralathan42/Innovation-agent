# Funding report: contactless radar screening for sleep apnea (obstructive vs central)

Report date: 2026-11-25. The disclosure is fictional demo material. I searched with generic terms only (radar, sleep apnea, contactless sleep monitoring), so no distinctive details left this computer.

## 1. Where the invention stands and what the next step needs

**Stage.** This is a working prototype with first clinical evidence, roughly TRL 4–5. In plain terms, it has been shown to work in a hospital setting, but it is not yet a product.
- The test covered 46 nights with 31 patients, compared with polysomnography.
- AHI agreed within 4 events per hour on 91% of nights.
- Obstructive and central events were told apart correctly 87% of the time.
- The prototype costs about EUR 60 in parts.

**Gaps.**
- **IP.** Nothing is filed and nothing is published. A conference paper is planned for June 2027. A priority filing must be made before that paper, and before any further public talks. The seminar talk that sparked company interest needs checking for what was disclosed. This is the most urgent item.
- **Clinical evidence.** The sample is small and from one site. Regulators and payers will want a larger multi-night, multi-site study with a pre-specified protocol. The hospital sleep unit already wants to run one.
- **Regulation.** Nobody on the team has MDR experience. The device is likely software plus hardware used for diagnosis, which probably means a Class IIa medical device with notified-body involvement. The AI classifier adds a further layer of requirements. The regulatory class needs to be confirmed with a consultant.
- **Company.** The founders are the doctoral researcher and the postdoc. The doctoral researcher completed the Aalto Ventures Program and wants to found after the PhD. The professor stays in academia.
- **Partners.** Two Finnish health tech companies have shown interest, and the hospital is a clinical partner.

**What the next step needs (my estimate, to be validated).**
- **Money:** about EUR 0.5–0.8 million over 12–18 months. This covers the IP filing, a pre-product design iteration, a multi-site validation study, a regulatory strategy, and a founding team. A full CE-marked product needs several million more later.
- **Partners:** the university hospital sleep unit, a second clinical site, a regulatory and quality consultant, and ideally one of the two interested companies as a pilot or distribution partner.
- **Time:** 12–18 months to a validated, regulation-ready prototype. CE marking would follow, typically another 12–24 months.

## 2. Best-fitting funding now

Calls in our data and on the web were mostly checked against our own library notes (health-tech memory.md), not the full call texts. Eligibility and dates need confirming with each funder.

**1. Business Finland Research to Business (R2B)**
- **Funder and programme:** Business Finland, Research to Business.
- **Size:** funds 80% of costs. Typical budgets are EUR 500,000–800,000 (from our library, health-tech memory).
- **Deadline:** call 1/2027 dates are not published. Call 1/2026 ran 2025-12-01 to 2026-03-04, so expect a December 2026 opening and an early-March 2027 close. I could not confirm this. Call 2/2026 closed 2026-09-16, so it is too late.
- **Eligibility:** a research organisation applies for a project that prepares commercialisation of a research-based solution, and it often leads to a spin-off. It requires the invention to be unpublished or protected. Aalto Innovation should confirm the details.
- **Fit:** the best fit overall. It suits the stage, the spin-off plan, and the clinical and regulatory work. Strong, but the exact dates are the open risk.
- **Link:** [R2B](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/funding-services/cooperation-between-companies-and-research-organisations/research-to-business/), [call 2/2026 page](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/r2b-funding-call-2-2026/). The contact on the call pages is Norma Saha (norma.saha@businessfinland.fi).

**2. EIC Accelerator 2027 (short proposal, then full proposal)**
- **Funder and programme:** European Innovation Council.
- **Size:** grants up to EUR 2.5 million plus equity.
- **Deadline:** short proposals can be submitted any time until 2027-12-16. Full-proposal cut-offs are 2027-01-20, 2027-03-17, 2027-09-01 and 2027-11-04.
- **Eligibility:** for a single SME, or a team that will found one. A company is normally needed by contract time. Check with the funder. Applicants usually need to show strong evidence of the market and the team.
- **Fit:** good in size, but premature now. It fits after the IP filing, a company, a larger study, and regulatory planning. It is a realistic target for the 2027-09-01 or 2027-11-04 cut-offs.
- **Link:** [EIC Accelerator](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en).

**3. EIT Health Innovation Validation 2027**
- **Funder and programme:** EIT Health.
- **Size:** up to EUR 850,000 per project at 50% co-funding. Of this, EUR 400,000–500,000 goes to the commercialising company.
- **Deadline:** 2027-01-13. That is about seven weeks away.
- **Eligibility:** a company must lead. Micro and small leads need 4 FTEs and EUR 250,000 raised. Target is digital or AI medical devices at IML 6.
- **Fit:** poor for now, because the team has no company, no FTEs and no funding raised. Keep it in mind for 2028 once a company exists.
- **Link:** [EIT Health Innovation Validation 2027](https://www.eit.europa.eu/our-activities/opportunities/innovation-validation-call-2027).

**4. Horizon Europe HLTH 2027, AI predictive biomarkers and point-of-care diagnostics**
- **Funder and programme:** Horizon Europe, Cluster Health.
- **Size:**
  - Point-of-care diagnostics: EUR 39.3 million, about 6 grants of EUR 5–7 million.
  - AI predictive biomarkers: EUR 44.2 million, about 6 grants of EUR 6–8 million.
- **Deadline:** stage 1 on 2027-02-17, stage 2 on 2027-09-16.
- **Eligibility:** a consortium. The point-of-care topic requires clinical studies and conformity assessment, so it is a large project.
- **Fit:** partial. The device is a home or point-of-care screening tool and might fit the diagnostics topic, but the grants are large and the call is for mature, consortium-led work. The team would be a junior partner. The biomarker topic excludes cancer and targets chronic non-communicable diseases, and I have not read it against sleep apnea.
- **Link:** [point-of-care diagnostics](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-hlth-2027-02-ind-02-two-stage), [AI predictive biomarkers](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-hlth-2027-02-tool-01-two-stage).

## 5. Recommended funding path

**Before any application (now to January 2027).**
1. Check what the seminar talk disclosed. File a priority patent application, with the Aalto Innovation patent team, before the June 2027 paper and before more talks.
2. Agree with the hospital sleep unit on a larger-study protocol and on data rights.
3. Ask one or both interested companies for a letter of support and a co-funding commitment, which R2B favours. Sign a confidentiality agreement first.
4. Find a medical device regulatory adviser, even part-time, and write a one-page regulatory plan: intended use, classification, and the AI and software documentation route.
5. Contact Business Finland about R2B dates and fit.

**Timeline.**

| When | Step |

| Dec 2026 – early Mar 2027 | R2B call 1/2027 (expected, unconfirmed). Decision likely about 3 months later. |

If R2B fails or is delayed, the fallback is a Business Finland or Aalto early-stage programme plus a company-funded pilot with one of the Finnish partners. Take a second R2B attempt in the next call (autumn 2027, dates not published).

**What the team must have in place first.**
- A filed patent application.
- A signed agreement on ownership between Aalto, the inventors and the hospital data. Aalto Innovation should confirm.
- A clinical study plan with the hospital.
- At least one partner letter.
- A regulatory plan and adviser.
- A founding team decision, because the doctoral researcher is not planning to found until after the PhD, and EIC and EIT Health money depends on having a company.
