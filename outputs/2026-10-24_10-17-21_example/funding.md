# Funding report: water-based lignin binder for Li-ion cathodes (NMC811)

*Prepared by Aalto Innovation, 24 October 2026. The disclosure is confidential: all searches used only general terms such as "lignin", "battery binder" and "PFAS-free".*

---

## 1. Where the invention stands and what the next step needs

**Technology readiness: about TRL 3–4.** The concept works in the lab. Coin cells keep 91% of capacity after 500 cycles (PVDF: 89%), peel strength matches PVDF, and the binder has been made in 50 g batches. Nothing has been tested at cell or production scale yet.

**Why it matters to the market:**
- The binder removes the toxic NMP solvent and the PVDF fluoropolymer from cathode making.
- Drying needs less energy.
- It uses a cheap side-stream from Finnish pulp mills.
- The EU's proposed PFAS restriction puts pressure on PVDF. One Swedish-funded project describes this as a "PFAS ban by 2030", which needs checking against the current ECHA proposal.
- The fluorine in PVDF comes from fluorspar, which is on the EU critical raw materials list. That is a useful argument for EU calls.

**What is missing:**

| Gap | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Patent protection** | No publication yet, but a manuscript is planned for spring 2027. **A priority application must be filed before then**, and before any non-confidential talks with partners. |
| **Industrially relevant data** | Battery makers will want: pouch cells (≥1 Ah), stability at high voltage (≥4.3 V; the literature reports lignin redox side-reactions between 2.8 and 4.5 V), slurry rheology and coating on a pilot coater, thick electrodes, kg-scale binder synthesis, batch-to-batch consistency with industrial kraft lignin, and a techno-economic assessment and life-cycle analysis (TEA/LCA). |
| **Industry partners** | The pulp company's interest is informal, and there is no battery maker or cathode producer contact. EU calls and Business Finland both reward letters of intent. |
| **Business lead** | The postdoc wants to continue, but nobody with business experience is involved. Research to Business (R2B) and later Deep Tech Accelerator (DTA) and EIC funding all expect a commercial lead. |

**Next stage (about 18–24 months, about €500–700k):** scale binder synthesis from 50 g to 1 kg+, make pouch cells, test coating on a pilot line (for example at VTT or a European battery pilot line; to be checked), run early TEA/LCA, validate the market with 10–20 cathode and cell makers, and decide whether to found a spin-off. After that, a spin-off or consortium phase (TRL 5–7) needs about €2–5M and an industrial cell partner.

## 5. Recommended funding path

**Before applying anywhere (Nov–Dec 2026):**
1. **IPR first.** Aalto Innovation reviews the invention and searches prior art (the patent and papers above, plus the UEF project's publications). File a **priority patent application by about Q1 2027**, well before the spring 2027 manuscript. Use NDAs in all talks with partners.
2. **Commercial lead.** Recruit a business lead, for example through Aalto's networks of entrepreneurs-in-residence and experienced founders. The postdoc becomes the technical founder.
3. **Letters of intent.** Turn the pulp company's informal interest into a letter of intent. Approach 2–3 cathode or cell makers or pilot lines for a letter of intent or material-testing agreement. Also contact Stora Enso.
4. **Quick extra data, if possible:** a first pouch cell, a check at 4.3 V, and a 0.5–1 kg batch. These strengthen every application.

**Application sequence:**

| Timing | Action | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 2026 | Attend a Business Finland R2B Clinic. Check the 1/2027 call dates. Contact the UEF group about working together on EU calls. | — |
| Dec 2026 – Jan 2027 | Join a CL5-D2-06 consortium as a materials partner (optional, low effort) or a MAT-PROD-06 consortium (only if one is already forming; deadline 2 Feb 2027). | Partner share about €0.3–0.8M |
| **About Mar 2027 (check)** | **Submit the R2B application** (core funding for 18–24 months of validation, market work and spin-off preparation). | About €500–700k total, 80% grant |
| **14 Apr 2027** | **CL5-D2-07 stage 1** (blind short proposal), as work-package lead in a European consortium (Area A, low cost) | About €4.8M project, Aalto about €0.5M+ |
| About May 2027 (check) | EIC Pathfinder Open 2027, only if there is a strong, novel cross-border consortium story | Up to about €3M (check) |
| Sept 2027 | CIRCBIO-09 with the pulp partner (optional). Watch for an EIT RawMaterials 2027 call. | €6M IA |
| 7 Oct 2027 | CL5-D2-07 stage 2, if stage 1 passes | — |
| **2028** (end of R2B) | **Found the spin-off.** Then apply for Business Finland Sprint or DTA, EIT RawMaterials Booster or InnoEnergy, and pre-seed investors (Finnish deep-tech or climate funds). | €0.4–1.2M+ |
| 2029+ | EIC Accelerator, once there is pilot data from a cell maker (TRL 6) | Up to €2.5M grant + equity |

**Main risks:**
- The UEF project, WOODCELL and the Swedish efforts mean the window for differentiating the invention is short. File the patent and publish quickly afterwards.
- Lignin's stability at high voltage on NMC811 is the main technical question cell makers will ask.
- Public funding is limited in 2027: there is no Research Council PoC call, the Business Finland budget has been cut, and 2027 is the last year of Horizon Europe. That makes R2B and the CL5 battery topics the key bets.

**Dates that need checking with the funder:**
- R2B 1/2027 dates (my estimate from earlier calls).
- EIC Pathfinder 2027 deadline and terms (Work Programme expected Nov 2026).
- Future DTA and Sprint rounds.
- EIT RawMaterials and InnoEnergy 2027 calls, and Aalto's EIT partner status.
- Liikesivistysrahasto 2027 round.
- EU topic deadlines: confirm on the [Funding & Tenders Portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/) before committing.
