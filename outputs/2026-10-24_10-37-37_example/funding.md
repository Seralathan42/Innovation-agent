# Funding report: water-based lignin binder for Li-ion cathodes

**Prepared by:** Aalto Innovation, funding advisory · **Date:** 24 October 2026 · **Confidential**

**Summary:** The best next step is a full **Business Finland Research to Business (R2B)** project. The spring 2027 call is expected to open around December 2026, but the date is not yet confirmed. Before applying, two things must happen: a **priority patent application has to be filed** before anything is published, and the team needs **a business lead and letters of interest from industry**. EU battery calls (BATT4EU) are a useful second track as a consortium partner. Spin-off funding (Business Finland Sprint, EIC) comes after R2B, roughly 2028–2029.

**Note on searches:** I searched using only general terms (lignin binder, battery binder, water-based cathode). No distinctive details from the disclosure were used.

---

## 1. Where the invention stands and what the next step needs

**Maturity: about TRL 3–4.** The concept is proven in the lab.
- Coin cells hold 91% of capacity after 500 cycles, against 89% for PVDF. Peel strength is similar to PVDF.
- All work so far is lab scale: coin cells and 50 g batches of binder.
- There are no pouch or multilayer cells yet, no tests on a real coating line, no techno-economic analysis (TEA) or life-cycle assessment (LCA), and no data on how the binder ages in storage or varies between lignin batches.

**Why the timing is good:**
- The EU's planned restriction of PFAS chemicals would cover PVDF, so battery makers need fluorine-free binders.
- EU rules on NMP and energy costs make solvent-free or water-based processing attractive.
- Lignin is a cheap by-product from Finnish pulp mills.

**What needs attention first:**

| Issue | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **IP** | A journal article is planned for spring 2027. A **priority patent application must be filed before** that article, any conference talk, or any open part of a funding proposal. This is the first task for Aalto Innovation. |
| **Team** | The postdoc wants to continue with the technology. The professor will stay in academia. Nobody has business experience. R2B and later investors will expect a commercial lead, such as an entrepreneur-in-residence or an experienced business person. |
| **Market proof** | There is informal interest from a pulp company on supply, but **no contact with battery makers**. Finland has electrode-material and cell-related companies, and Nordic cell makers exist. Letters of interest from customers are essential. Note that Northvolt, which earlier anchored Nordic binder projects, went bankrupt in 2025. |

**What the next stage (TRL 5–6) needs:**
- **Technical work (18–24 months):**
  - scale up binder synthesis from 50 g to 1–10 kg
  - check that lignin batches behave consistently
  - test slurry behaviour and coating at pilot scale, for example at VTT or a European battery pilot line
  - build multilayer pouch cells of at least 1 Ah and test them for 1,000+ cycles
  - prepare TEA and LCA comparing the binder with PVDF/NMP
  - draft the freedom-to-operate (FTO) analysis
- **Money:** about €500k–800k for a research-led R2B-type project. If the spin-off route continues, a further €1–3M after that.
- **Partners:** a lignin supplier (already interested), at least one cathode or cell maker, or a pilot-line operator, for testing; a business lead.

## 5. Recommended funding path

**Before applying anywhere (Nov 2026 – Jan 2027):**
1. **File a priority patent application**, aiming for Dec 2026 – Jan 2027. Before that, run a novelty search covering the UEF and Swedish work and the published kraft-lignin binder papers. Hold back the spring 2027 manuscript until the application is filed.
2. **Find a business lead.** Options include an entrepreneur-in-residence through Aalto Innovation, an experienced person from the battery industry, or an Aalto Startup Center coach.
3. **Get letters of interest.** One letter of intent from the pulp company. At least one or two letters from a cathode, electrode or cell maker, ideally including an offer to test samples. Sign NDAs before sharing data.
4. **Clarify roles and IP.** The postdoc's role and contract cover through to R2B. The professor is an inventor and adviser but not part of the company. Confirm that the Academy of Finland project and the R2B pre-study place no restrictions on the IP.
5. **Contact EU consortia** for the BATT4EU topics D2-06 and D2-07. Business Finland's Horizon Europe advisers and the Aalto research services team can help.

**Timeline:**

|---|---|---|
| Nov 2026 – Jan 2027 | Patent filing, business lead, letters of interest, EU consortium search. Fortum and Neste Foundation or similar grant for a doctoral student if the timing works. | Internal funds; up to €27k/year from a foundation |
| **About Dec 2026 – Mar 2027** | **Apply for a full R2B project** (main application): kg-scale synthesis, pouch cells, pilot coating, TEA and LCA, business model, spin-off plan | €500k–800k total, 80% grant |

- If R2B fails in spring 2027, reapply in autumn 2027 using the reviewers' feedback.
- If the spin-off route stalls, licensing to the pulp company or a binder maker is still possible, with an industry-funded research project as the next step.

**Everything to confirm with the funders before relying on it:**
