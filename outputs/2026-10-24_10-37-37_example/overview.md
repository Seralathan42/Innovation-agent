# Overview: water-based lignin binder for Li-ion cathodes (NMC811)

*Disclosure dated 15 Sep 2026 (marked as a fictional test example). Confidential. Based only on the disclosure and the NABC, IP, market and funding reports.*

## 1. Verdict
This is promising but at an early stage (TRL 3–4). It addresses a real problem: replacing PVDF and NMP, the toxic solvent PVDF needs. The weak points are that lignin cathode binders are already patented and published, and the team has no business lead. Any patent value depends on the specific chemical modification and how it performs on NMC811, and the disclosure doesn't describe the modification. Next, get that description, commission a professional novelty search and freedom-to-operate (FTO) check, and decide on a priority filing before the spring 2027 manuscript. In parallel, build a full Business Finland R2B application.

**Route to market:** the NABC and market reports favour licensing. The funding report sets out a path to a spin-off in 2028–29, with licensing as the fallback.

## 3. Biggest risks
- **Patentability and FTO:** the broad idea is already covered by earlier work, and the RISE patent may cover the Aalto lignin if its process removes low-molecular-weight fractions.
- **Losing novelty:** the spring 2027 manuscript, conference talks, theses, open parts of funding proposals or pre-study reports could make the invention public before a patent is filed.
- **Technical:** water processing of NMC811 causes lithium leaching, rising slurry pH and aluminium foil corrosion. There is no data yet from pouch cells, industrial loadings or comparisons between lignin batches.
- **Commercial:** no business lead, no interest yet from cell makers, dry coating as a competing route, and qualification cycles that take years.

## 5. Questions for the researchers
1. What exactly is the chemical modification? Does your process remove low-molecular-weight lignin fractions?
2. How many cells did you test, how much did the results vary, and at what loading? Do you have any pouch-cell or high-voltage data?
3. How do you deal with lithium leaching, slurry pH and aluminium corrosion? Did you use carbon-coated foil?
4. Has anything been shared outside the group, for example at conferences, in theses or in Business Finland reports? Do the funders' terms or any agreement with the pulp company (such as an MTA) affect who owns the IP?
5. Does the postdoc want to found a spin-off or be the technical lead in a licensing deal? Would they work with a business co-founder?
