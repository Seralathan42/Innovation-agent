# Funding report: self-calibrating acoustic leak detection for district heating pipes

The disclosure is a fictional test case. Search terms were kept generic and no distinctive details were used.

## 1. Where the invention stands and what the next step needs

**Stage (about TRL 4–5).**
- A lab rig (DN100, 40 m) detected 0.5 l/min leaks with position error under 1 m.
- A 4-month field trial on 2.3 km of network with 6 sensors found 3 of 3 known leaks.
- The mean position error was 2.1 m, against 9.4 m for a manual correlator on the same data.
- There were 2 false alarms, both from a pump station.
- The sensors are off-the-shelf builds at about €120 each.
- Battery life of about 5 years is only an estimate.

**What is missing.**
- There is no patent search or filing.
- Only one network has been tested, and only 3 leaks were found.
- The sensor is not an industrial design. It still needs an ATEX/IP/EMC review and a check of clamp-on installation in valve chambers.
- There is no market study, and only one company has been contacted.
- There is no company and no business expertise in the team.

**Urgent point: IP.**
- The conference paper was submitted on 15 September 2026, and the decision is due in December 2026.
- Novelty may be lost if the paper becomes public first.
- Aalto Innovation should run a quick patent search and file a priority application before any publication or any detailed talk with outside companies.
- Check that the energy company's test-network access agreement does not give it rights to the results.

**Next step: a larger pilot and commercialisation preparation.**
- Run a 2027 pilot of about 20–50 sensors over a full heating season, in more than one network and with more than one pipe type.
- Build an industrial-grade sensor prototype.
- Add false-alarm filtering.
- Run customer discovery with 5–10 district heating operators.
- Form a founding team, with a business lead and the doctoral researcher.

**Rough needs.**
- Money: about €400–700k over 18–24 months.
- Partners: the host energy company, ideally one or two more operators, and a hardware or manufacturing partner.
- Time: IP filing within weeks, and the pilot in the 2027–28 heating season.

## 2. Best-fitting funding now

Little is open that fits well right now. The best routes are Business Finland's Research to Business (R2B) and, for the company stage, startup funding. Most of the matches below are for later.

**1. Business Finland Research to Business (R2B).** This is the strongest fit.
- **Funder and size:** Business Finland. It is a grant of 80% of eligible costs, with total project costs typically €400–700k and projects of 1.5–2 years.
- **Deadline:** Autumn 2026 closed on 16 September 2026, with decisions in January 2027. The next round is probably in early March 2027 (my estimate from past patterns). Confirm the date with Business Finland.
- **Eligibility:** The applicant must be a research organisation in Finland, so Aalto applies. The project needs a clear commercialisation plan, a founder or team ready to take it on, and market and IP groundwork. Business Finland expects a path to a company or licensing deal.
- **Fit:** Good. The technology is validated and a customer is interested. The weak points are no IP filing and no business lead.
- **Links:** [Call 2/2026](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/r2b-funding-call-2-2026/) and [Call 1/2026](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/research-to-business--funding-call-12026/).

**2. Business Finland Sprint funding (pilot) and R&D funding for a new company.**
- **Programme:** Tempo has been closed to new applications. Business Finland says Sprint is being piloted as first R&D funding for new startups.
- **Eligibility:** The applicant must be a company, so this is for after the spin-off is founded. The terms need checking with the funder.
- **Fit:** Later, after the company exists.
- **Link:** [Business Finland news on 2026 services](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/whats-new/news/2026/what-do-business-finlands-funding-services-look-like-at-the-beginning-of-2026/).

**3. Research Council of Finland (RCF) Proof of Concept.**
- **Size:** Up to €300k for a single project and €450k for a consortium.
- **Deadline:** The 2026 call closed on 6 May 2026. One source says no call will open in 2027. Verify on the RCF site.
- **Eligibility:** The principal investigator must lead an RCF project ending between 1 January 2025 and 31 December 2026. The team's RCF project runs to 2027, so it is probably not eligible. Check with the RCF.
- **Fit:** Low.
- **Link:** [2026 RCF Proof of Concept call](https://www.aka.fi/en/research-funding/apply-for-funding/calls-for-applications/apply-now2/2026-rcf-proof-of-concept-funding/).

**4. EIC Accelerator.**
- **Programme:** Horizon Europe, for companies. The short-proposal deadline is today, 4 November 2026 (HORIZON-EIC-2026-ACCELERATOR-01).
- **Fit:** Not realistic. There is no company yet, and the invention is early for it.
- **Link:** [EU call search](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-search).

**5. Horizon Europe "Proof of market" (HORIZON-CL4-2027-01-MAT-PROD-49).**
- **Size:** About €200k per grant, with €4.7m in total. It is open now and closes on 2 February 2027.
- **Eligibility:** It only covers results from earlier Horizon Cluster 4 or Horizon 2020 LEIT projects.
- **Fit:** No. The invention comes from an RCF project, so it is not eligible.
- **Link:** [Call page](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-cl4-2027-01-mat-prod-49).

**6. Pilot co-funding from the energy company (in kind or cash).**
- The host company asked about a 2027 pilot. Its support can serve as the company contribution in Business Finland applications and as a letter of commitment.
- Not a formal grant, so no link.

## 4. Who has been funded for similar work

These are the results from the project database. Few relate directly to district heating.
- **Smart Leak Detection Pipes**, UKRI, [EP/K504191/1](https://gtr.ukri.org/projects?ref=EP%2FK504191%2F1), £240,685, 2013–2017. Self-contained sensor nodes clamped on water pipes, detecting vibration and pressure. This is a close technical analogue and a prior-art check.
- **Limpet, continuous pipeline leak detection**, Innovate UK, project 830047, £649,637, 2018–2021. A commercial development example and a possible competitor.
- **Acoustic Sensing on Gas and Oil Pipelines for Leak Detection**, UKRI, project 2108480, 2018–2022. Academic work on acoustic pipeline sensing.
- **Real-time water leak detection using digital twins**, FCT Portugal, project 2023.02917.BDANA, 2023–2026. A data-driven alternative approach.
- **Autonomous Wireless Leak Detection**, Research Council of Norway, project 241526, 2014–2015. Small funding.
- **District heating storage research**, RCF, project 295520, €2,000. This is the only district heating hit, and it concerns a different topic.

I did not find a direct competitor for self-calibrating active-pulse acoustic detection in this search, but the search was shallow. Fixed acoustic loggers sold by water-leak vendors are the likely competitors. They also make good licensing or distribution partners. A proper patent and market search is needed. No links were returned for most of these projects. Search the project codes in the UKRI Gateway to Research or OpenAIRE.

## 5. Recommended funding path

**Now to December 2026: secure IP and the base.**
1. Run a patent search and file a priority application before the paper decision or any publication.
2. Check the agreement with the energy company on IP and on data rights.
3. Talk to Aalto Innovation about internal pre-seed or ideation support, and about foundations. I could not verify these.

**December 2026 to March 2027: prepare R2B.**
4. Interview 5–10 district heating operators and get letters of intent for the pilot.
5. Name a business lead or mentor. The team has none, and R2B reviewers will ask about this.
6. Plan the pilot, sensor industrialisation and false-alarm filtering, with a budget of €400–700k.
7. Submit R2B in the spring 2027 round, if the date is confirmed. Use the autumn 2027 round as the fallback.

**2027 to 2028: pilot and spin-off.**
8. If R2B is granted (decision about 3–4 months after the deadline), run the pilot with the host company.
9. Found the company after the doctorate (expected 2027), and license or assign the IP from Aalto.
10. Then apply for Business Finland Sprint or R&D funding for the company, plus angel or seed investors. Consider the EIC Accelerator once there is pilot data.

**Must be in place first:** the patent application, a business lead, a customer letter of support, clear IP ownership (Aalto and the two inventors, and the host company's position), and a pilot plan with costs.

**Checks needed with the funders:** the R2B 2027 dates and eligibility, whether any RCF Proof of Concept call exists in 2027, and the Sprint terms.
