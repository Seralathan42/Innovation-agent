# Funding report: water-based lignin binder for Li-ion cathodes (NMC811)

Date of report: 2026-11-25. The disclosure is confidential. Web searches used only general terms such as "lignin battery binder" and "battery materials". Findings from our own data are marked **(own data)**.

## 1. Where the invention stands and what the next step needs

**Stage.** This is a lab-proven concept, roughly TRL 3–4.
- Coin cells keep 91% of capacity after 500 cycles, against 89% for PVDF.
- Peel strength is similar to PVDF.
- Binder batches are only 50 g.
- Nothing has been tested in pouch cells, at pilot scale or by a battery maker.
- There is no publication yet (manuscript planned for spring 2027).
- The earlier Business Finland Research to Business (R2B) pre-study has ended.

**Gaps to close before a company or licence deal is credible:**
1. **Patent position.** I could not tell from the disclosure whether a patent application exists. Filing must come before the manuscript is published, and also before any outside talks beyond what an NDA covers.
2. **Independent validation.** The key test is NMC811 pouch cells, with a third-party or industrial lab confirming results against the PVDF/NMP baseline. Aqueous processing of Ni-rich cathodes is a known hard problem, so the team should test:
   - slurry stability and pH
   - Ni-rich cathode surface degradation
   - aluminium foil corrosion
   - drying and coating quality
3. **Scale-up of the binder.** The next step is 1–10 kg batches with the pulp company, plus a cost model and a view of lignin supply quality.
4. **A battery-maker contact.** There is none yet. A cell maker or cathode-coating company needs to define the specification and run test coating.
5. **A business lead.** The postdoc wants to continue, but nobody has business experience. A spin-off needs a business co-founder or an experienced chair.

**Rough needs (my estimates; check with the funder or advisers):**
- Validation, patenting and a business case: EUR 150–400k over 9–12 months.
- Pilot-scale pouch cell demonstration: EUR 0.5–1.5M over 18–30 months, usually with industrial partners.
- Seed-round money for a spin-off after that.

**Partners.** Pulp company (lignin supplier), a cathode or cell maker, a pilot coating line (for example a Finnish or Nordic battery pilot facility) and an independent test lab.

## 2. Best-fitting funding now

**1. Business Finland Co-Research (research organisations with SMEs)**
- Funder and programme: Business Finland, [Cooperation between research organizations and SMEs](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/call-research-organizations-and-smes/).
- Deadline: **2026-12-18** (own data, last checked 2026-11-19).
- Size and eligibility: research organisations are funded at 80%. The steering group needs at least 3 SMEs. No total budget is published.
- Fit: moderate. It suits a 2–3 year research project that takes the binder towards pilot scale, with the pulp company and battery or coating SMEs as the company group.
- Concern: 3 weeks is very short to assemble 3 committed SMEs, and the pulp company may be a large company. **Check with Business Finland whether the company mix qualifies.** If the deadline cannot be met, ask about the next call, or whether this route is better placed alongside the Academy of Finland project.

Call 2/2026 closed 2026-09-16 and had EUR 7.5M ([call page](https://www.businessfinland.fi/en/services/funding/calls/2026/r2b-funding-call-2-2026/)). **3. Horizon Europe, battery concepts (Batt4EU/IAM4EU)**
- Funder and programme: EU, [HORIZON-CL5-2027-04-Two-Stage-D2-07](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/horizon-cl5-2027-04-two-stage-d2-07), a Research and Innovation Action.
- Size and dates: EUR 48M in total, about 10 grants of EUR 4.8M. It opens 2026-12-15. The first stage is due 2027-04-14 and the second 2027-10-07. TRL 4–5 is expected at the end of the project, and activities may start at any TRL. I read the topic text via the portal.
- Fit: moderate to good, as a work package within a consortium.
  - Area A (low-cost chemistries, with main component cost under EUR 35/kWh and the use of fewer critical raw materials) and the call's safe-and-sustainable-by-design language match a PVDF/NMP-free binder.
  - Aalto would join as a partner. It is unlikely to lead.
  - First-stage proposals are blind.
- Eligibility: consortium rules apply. **Check them in the portal.**

## 4. Who has been funded for similar work

The OpenAIRE project search was thin. It returned nothing for "binder cathode" under EC funding and only two lignin and battery projects. Absence of hits does not mean no competition.

- **BALIHT**, a fully lignin-based organic redox flow battery. EU, 2019–2023, EUR 4,098,550. The topic is related, but it is lignin as an active material in flow batteries, not a binder. Possible contacts for lignin chemistry know-how. [Project record via OpenAIRE](https://explore.openaire.eu/search/find?keyword=BALIHT)
- **BATTERFLAI**, lignin nanoparticles as flotation collectors for battery minerals. Vinnova (Sweden), SEK 300,000 for the EIT RawMaterials KAVA 6 proposal, 2019. The proposal was reported as granted. Weak relevance, but it shows EIT RawMaterials has funded lignin and battery work. [Project record via OpenAIRE](https://explore.openaire.eu/search/find?keyword=BATTERFLAI)
- **Aalto battery work (own data):** a Business Finland solid-state lithium battery project (2021–2024, EUR 890,000). Halide Energy is an Aalto copper flow battery spinout and a useful example of the spin-off route. Tanja Kallio's electrochemical group works on electrode manufacturing, so it is a natural internal collaborator or test partner.

**Possible partners and competitors (own data):**
- **Stora Enso (FI):** forest-products group with lignin binder and biomaterial work and 194 stored patents. It may be both a possible lignin supplier or partner and a competitor, since it is already active in lignin-based battery materials. This should be checked in a freedom-to-operate review before any contact.
- **LignoSphere (FI):** Aalto and Häme UAS origin, colloidal lignin particles, raised over EUR 1.2 million in 2026. It is a Finnish lignin spin-off and a useful model for the team, and possibly a collaborator. It is also a potential competitor for the same investors.
- **Lignin Industries AB (SE)** and **Liquid Lignin Co (US):** lignin material companies in our database. They have few stored patents, but they are possible competitors or alternative lignin sources.
- **Battery materials in Finland (own data):** Umicore (Kokkola), a Chinese-Finnish cathode plant under construction in Kotka, and CeLLife Technologies (Tampere). These are the nearest cathode-side contacts for testing and customer discovery. The Finnish battery strategy is moving towards materials, which is a favourable setting.

## 5. Recommended funding path

**Before applying for anything (now to January 2027):**
1. **Protect the IP.** Check with Aalto Innovation whether a patent application is filed, and file before any publication or external presentation. Move the manuscript date if needed. Sign NDAs before talking to the pulp company and battery makers.
2. **Name a business lead.** Find a business co-founder or a mentor through Aalto Startup Center or Aalto Innovation. Decide with the postdoc whether the aim is a spin-off or a licence. The professor stays as scientific adviser.
3. **Collect letters of intent** from the pulp company (lignin supply) and 1–2 cell or cathode companies.
4. **Run a freedom-to-operate and prior-art check** against Stora Enso and other lignin binder patents.

**Timeline:**
- **Dec 2026:**
  - Decide on Business Finland Co-Research (deadline 2026-12-18). Go only if 3 SMEs can really commit. Otherwise skip it.
  - Ask Business Finland about the next R2B round and about the best route after a pre-study.
- **Dec 2026 – Mar 2027:** Hold a short consortium search for Horizon D2-07 (stage 1 due 2027-04-14), approaching battery research groups and cell makers through the BATT4EU network. Aalto's role would be the binder work package. The decision on whether to proceed is a go/no-go for January.
- **Spring 2027 (date unconfirmed):** Apply for R2B or the closest successor, with a validation plan for pouch cells, a market study and a business plan. The R2B grant would pay for validation, patent costs and customer discovery.
- **Mid 2027:** File the manuscript. Possibly a second Horizon try (D2-07 stage 2 on 2027-10-07, or the bio-based calls on 2027-09-22).
- **2027–2028:** If pouch-cell validation is positive and a customer is interested, found the company. Then apply for DTA (Business Finland) and seek seed money. Later options are EIC Accelerator and Finnish deep tech investors. I did not search investors in detail, so this needs a separate search.

**Cautions:**
- All dates are from the EU portal and Business Finland pages as of 2026-11-25. **Check eligibility and dates with the funder** before committing, especially for Co-Research, the next R2B round, CBE JU and DTA.
- The Academy of Finland project is already funding the team, so check overlap and double-funding rules with each funder.
- Sector context from our energy library (own data): Finland's battery strategy now favours materials over cell manufacturing, and the EU relies on China for 90% of imported Li-ion cells. Both help the case for a locally sourced, NMP-free binder.
